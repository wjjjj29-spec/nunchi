How it works

  1. You tell us where and when. Name of the place, date, time, how many people. A link helps but isn't needed.
  2. We book it under our Korean account. CatchTable, Naver, or a phone call in Korean. We put your name on the booking.
  3. You get a confirmation email with the time, the address in Korean for the taxi, and anything the venue told us (deposit, dress code, "last order at 9").
  4. You pay $12 by PayPal. Only for confirmed bookings. If a place is walk-in only or fully booked on all your dates, there's no charge.

Questions people ask

Which restaurants can you book?

Anything that takes reservations: CatchTable (the Korean app, not only Global), Naver Booking, Tabling, Poing, and places that only answer the phone. If it's walk-in only, we tell you and don't charge.

Can you get the really hot tables?

Sometimes. Places that release tables at a set time sell out in seconds, even for Koreans. We queue at the drop and try. Those attempts are $5 each, win or lose, and we tell you the odds honestly before you decide.

Do you need my passport or a Korean number?

No. We book under our account and give the venue your name. Bring your passport to the venue in case they check it.

What about deposits?

Some restaurants charge a deposit through the app. We'll tell you the amount before booking; you pay it to us with the fee and we cover it on the app. No-show deposits are not refundable.

Can you cancel or change a booking?

Yes, email us. Changes are free once. Cancelling late may forfeit a deposit, following the venue's rules.
