Thomas in Korean 토머스

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Thomas as 토머스: three syllables, said to-meo-seu. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Pronunciation tip

스 carries the filler vowel ㅡ (eu). Keep it short and light, almost just the consonant.

  • ㅓ (eo) in 머 is the open "uh" of "sun", not an "o".
  • ㅌ at the start is aspirated: a clear puff of air, like the t in "top".
  • ㅗ is a pure "o" with rounded lips. Don't add the w-glide English puts in "go".
  • Give all 3 syllables the same weight. Korean has no strong stress, so it's to-meo-seu, evenly.

Customer, client, online
토머스 님
토머스 nim. What you'll see on a delivery box or a booking.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables (after a one-syllable family name), so at 3 syllables 토머스 reads as a foreign name at a glance. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 토머스 or 토머스 씨.

Why it's spelled 토머스

Spelled letter-by-letter you'd get 소매스, but Korean media write 토머스, because the name is written the way it sounds, not the way it's spelled.

  • The th in Thomas is a plain t sound in English, so it's simply ㅌ (t).
  • The unstressed "uh" vowel is written ㅓ (eo), whatever letter English uses for it.
  • Korean adds the vowel ㅡ to consonants that have no vowel of their own (스), which is why Thomas has 3 syllables in Korean.

Questions people ask

How do you write Thomas in Korean?

Thomas is written 토머스 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: tomeoseu). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has three syllables: 토 + 머 + 스.

How do you pronounce Thomas in Korean?

Say it to-meo-seu, with even weight on every syllable. 스 carries the filler vowel ㅡ (eu). Keep it short and light, almost just the consonant.
