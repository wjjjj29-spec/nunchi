Austin in Korean 오스틴

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Austin as 오스틴: three syllables, said o-seu-tin. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Pronunciation tip

스 carries the filler vowel ㅡ (eu). Keep it short and light, almost just the consonant.

  • ㅗ is a pure "o" with rounded lips. Don't add the w-glide English puts in "go".
  • Give all 3 syllables the same weight. Korean has no strong stress, so it's o-seu-tin, evenly.

Customer, client, online
오스틴 님
오스틴 nim. What you'll see on a delivery box or a booking.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables (after a one-syllable family name), so at 3 syllables 오스틴 reads as a foreign name at a glance. In casual sentences Koreans often add 이 after a name that ends in a consonant: 오스틴이 왔어 ("Austin's here").

Why it's spelled 오스틴

Here the letters and the sound line up: applying the loanword rules letter by letter already gives 오스틴, the same spelling Korean media use.

  • Korean adds the vowel ㅡ to consonants that have no vowel of their own (스), which is why Austin has 3 syllables in Korean.

Questions people ask

How do you write Austin in Korean?

Austin is written 오스틴 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: oseutin). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has three syllables: 오 + 스 + 틴.

How do you pronounce Austin in Korean?

Say it o-seu-tin, with even weight on every syllable. 스 carries the filler vowel ㅡ (eu). Keep it short and light, almost just the consonant.
