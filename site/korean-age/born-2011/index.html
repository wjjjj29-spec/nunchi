Which age do Koreans actually use?

Since June 2023, laws and official documents use international age, so your passport age is your legal age in Korea too. But people still ask "몇 년생이에요?" (what year were you born?) instead of "how old are you?". Answer 11년생이에요 and everyone knows exactly where you fit, because in Korea the birth year, not the birthday, decides who is older.

Year age (15 in 2026) still decides when you can buy alcohol and cigarettes (from year age 19) and when men are called up for military service.

The 11-year-olds at school

Korean children born in 2011 started elementary school in March 2018 and in 2026 are in middle school year 3 (중3).

Who's older: oppa, unnie, hyung or noona?

Anyone born before 2011 is older than you in Korea, even if their birthday is in December and yours is in January. Someone born in 2011 too is your 동갑 (same age), and you can drop the honorifics.

Questions people ask

How old is someone born in 2011 in Korean age?

16 in 2026. Korean age is the current year minus the birth year, plus one.

What is the international age of someone born in 2011?

14 until their birthday in 2026, then 15.

What zodiac animal is 2011?

The Rabbit (토끼띠). If you were born in January or early February, before Lunar New Year, Koreans count you as the Tiger (호랑이띠) instead.
