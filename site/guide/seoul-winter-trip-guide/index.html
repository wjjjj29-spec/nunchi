Seoul in winter: what to pack, what's on and ski day trips

December to February: how cold it really gets, what to wear, the lights and the ₩1,000 ice rink, and how to reach the slopes from Seoul.

4 min readChecked 29 October 2026
At a glance1 / 6
The short version
  • Weather: average highs of 2–5°C and lows of −3 to −5.5°C from December to February, with January the coldest. Snow is light and patchy.
  • Pack: a long padded coat, thin thermal layers, hat, gloves and shoes with grip. Indoors is heated, so layer.
  • What's on: Seoul Winter Festa with lights and a Christmas market, and the Seoul Plaza ice rink at ₩1,000 an hour (2025–26 ran 19 December to 8 February).
  • Skiing: Vivaldi Park by shuttle in about 1.5 hours, Elysian Gangchon by train in under an hour, or Jisan in Icheon. 2026–27 opening dates weren't out by late October.

Seoul in winter is cold and mostly dry. From December to February, average highs sit between 2°C and 5°C and lows between −3°C and −5.5°C, with January the coldest month. Snow falls on a handful of days a month but rarely settles for long. Pack for cold wind, not deep snow, and you'll be fine: indoors is heated hard, the city puts on lights and a ₩1,000 ice rink, and three ski resorts are within a day trip.

These are the Korea Meteorological Administration's 1991–2020 averages. "Days with any snow" includes flurries and sleet. Averages hide cold snaps, and the dry northwesterly wind makes it feel colder than the number, so check the "feels like" temperature (체감온도, chegam ondo). For November and early December, see our Seoul weather guide for November and December.

What to pack

  • A warm padded coat, ideally long. Koreans switch to puffers once it gets cold, and the knee-length 롱패딩 (rong paeding) is everywhere.
  • Thermal base layers such as Heattech, called 발열내의 (baryeol naeui) here. Layer thin rather than bulky: subways, shops and cafés are warm, and you'll be unzipping every few minutes.
  • Hat, gloves, scarf and warm socks. Gloves that work with a touchscreen help when you're reading Naver Map on a cold street.
  • Shoes with grip. Pavements and stairs ice over after snow, especially on hilly streets.
  • Lip balm and moisturiser. Cold air outside and heated air inside dry everything out.

Heated floors and hand warmers

Many Korean homes, guesthouses and hanok stays heat through the floor, a system called 온돌 (ondol). The floor itself is warm, which is why Koreans take their shoes off and sit or sleep on it. Rooms can get hot and dry overnight, so turn the panel down before bed. If you get properly chilled, a jjimjilbang is the Korean answer: hot baths and heated floors for an evening or a night.

Every convenience store sells disposable hand warmers (핫팩, hatpaek) for about ₩1,300–1,500, often on 1+1 or 2+1 deals in winter. There are versions for your feet too. Our convenience store guide explains how those deals work.

Winter events in Seoul

  • Seoul Winter Festa. The city's winter festival last ran from 12 December 2025 to 4 January 2026: a Christmas market and carousel at Gwanghwamun Square, the Seoul Lantern Festival along Cheonggyecheon Stream, a New Year's Eve countdown at DDP and the midnight bell at Bosingak. The 2026 dates hadn't been announced by 23 October 2026, so check Seoul city's website from November.
  • Ice skating at Seoul Plaza. The rink in front of City Hall ran from 19 December 2025 to 8 February 2026. One hour costs ₩1,000, including skates and a helmet. It opens 10am–9:30pm Sunday to Friday and 10am–11pm on Saturdays and public holidays. Half the tickets for each session are sold online from 7 days ahead and half on the day. Discover Seoul Pass holders skate free.
  • Department store lights. Shinsegae's flagship on Sogong-ro, next to Myeongdong, turns its whole façade into a Christmas screen; in 2025 it started on 7 November. In 2025 Lotte ran a Christmas market on the lawn by Lotte World Tower in Jamsil from 20 November to 4 January, and The Hyundai Seoul in Yeouido built a Christmas village whose timed entry slots sold out in 30 minutes.

Christmas Day is a public holiday, and in 2026 it falls on a Friday. Check the dates of Christmas, New Year and Seollal on our public holidays calendar before you book.

Nunchi tip

Weekend evening sessions at the Seoul Plaza rink go fast. Book online exactly 7 days ahead, or go on a weekday morning, when the on-the-day tickets are easy to get.

  • Vivaldi Park is the biggest near Seoul, with 10 slopes and 10 lifts. In 2025–26 it also ran late-night skiing from 11pm to 3am, except Mondays, from 19 December to 17 February. Non-skiers can head to its Snowy Land snow park. Tour shuttles, bookable on sites such as Trazy, leave Hongik Univ. Station (Exit 8) at 7am and Euljiro 1-ga (Exit 8) at 7:30am, and come back at 6pm.
  • Elysian Gangchon calls itself the ski resort you reach by train. On winter weekends, seasonal ITX-Cheongchun trains from Yongsan, Wangsimni and Cheongnyangni stop at Baegyang-ri, and ordinary Gyeongchun Line trains stop there too. The slopes are a short walk from the station. The resort also runs free shuttle buses from points around Seoul, and tour shuttles with English-speaking staff leave from Hongdae, Dongdaemun, Gangnam and Jamsil and head back at 3:30pm.
  • Jisan Forest Resort, south-east of Seoul in Icheon, is popular for night skiing after work. In 2025–26 it joined the same season pass as Elysian Gangchon.

Lift tickets are sold by the hour (3, 5 or 7 hours is typical), and you can rent skis, boards and ski wear at the resorts. Tour shuttles close bookings a day or two before, and 2026–27 fares weren't listed when we checked in October, so look at the booking page. No resort near Seoul had announced its 2026–27 opening date by 20 October. Gangwon resorts usually open first, from late November, and those in Gyeonggi follow in early to mid December, depending on the cold.

Questions people ask

How cold is Seoul in winter?

From December to February, average highs are 2–5°C and lows −3 to −5.5°C (KMA 1991–2020 averages). January is the coldest month, and the dry wind makes it feel colder.

Does it snow in Seoul in winter?

Yes, but lightly. Seoul averages about 5–7 days a month with some snow from December to February, counting flurries and sleet. Heavy, lasting snow is unusual.

How much is the Seoul Plaza ice rink?

₩1,000 for one hour, including skates and a helmet. In 2025–26 it ran from 19 December to 8 February. Half the tickets are sold online from 7 days ahead and half on the day.

Can I go skiing as a day trip from Seoul?

Yes. Shuttle buses reach Vivaldi Park in about 1.5 hours, and Elysian Gangchon is under an hour by train to Baegyang-ri Station. Lift tickets are sold by the hour and gear is rented at the resort.

When is Seoul Winter Festa 2026?

The dates hadn't been announced by 23 October 2026. The last edition ran from 12 December 2025 to 4 January 2026, with the lantern festival on Cheonggyecheon and a market at Gwanghwamun.
