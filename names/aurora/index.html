Aurora in Korean 오로라

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Aurora as 오로라: three syllables, said o-ro-ra. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Pronunciation tip

ㄹ between two vowels (오로) is one quick tap of the tongue, like the tt in American "butter": closer to a soft d than an English r.

  • ㅗ is a pure "o" with rounded lips. Don't add the w-glide English puts in "go".
  • Give all 3 syllables the same weight. Korean has no strong stress, so it's o-ro-ra, evenly.

How Koreans would call you

Friends calling you over
오로라야!
오로라 ends in a vowel, so it takes 야. Casual, for close friends.
At work or with new people
오로라 씨
오로라 ssi. Polite, roughly "Mr" or "Ms", but used with the first name.
Customer, client, online
오로라 님
오로라 nim. What you'll see on a delivery box or a booking.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables (after a one-syllable family name), so at 3 syllables 오로라 reads as a foreign name at a glance. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 오로라 or 오로라 씨.

Why it's spelled 오로라

Here the letters and the sound line up: applying the loanword rules letter by letter already gives 오로라, the same spelling Korean media use.

Questions people ask

How do you write Aurora in Korean?

Aurora is written 오로라 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: orora). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has three syllables: 오 + 로 + 라.

How do you pronounce Aurora in Korean?

Say it o-ro-ra, with even weight on every syllable. ㄹ between two vowels (오로) is one quick tap of the tongue, like the tt in American "butter": closer to a soft d than an English r.
