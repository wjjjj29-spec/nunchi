Farhan in Korean 파르한

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Farhan as 파르한: three syllables, said pa-reu-han. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Pronunciation tip

ㄹ between two vowels (파르) is one quick tap of the tongue, like the tt in American "butter": closer to a soft d than an English r.

  • 르 carries the filler vowel ㅡ (eu). Keep it short and light, almost just the consonant.
  • ㅍ at the start is aspirated: a clear puff of air, like the p in "pie".
  • Give all 3 syllables the same weight. Korean has no strong stress, so it's pa-reu-han, evenly.

Spelled letter-by-letter you'd get 파핸, but Korean media write 파르한, because the name is written the way it sounds, not the way it's spelled.

  • Korean has no f sound, so f becomes ㅍ (p), said with a puff of air.
  • Korean adds the vowel ㅡ to consonants that have no vowel of their own (르), which is why Farhan has 3 syllables in Korean.

Questions people ask

How do you write Farhan in Korean?

Farhan is written 파르한 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: pareuhan). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has three syllables: 파 + 르 + 한.

How do you pronounce Farhan in Korean?

Say it pa-reu-han, with even weight on every syllable. ㄹ between two vowels (파르) is one quick tap of the tongue, like the tt in American "butter": closer to a soft d than an English r.

What does Farhan mean in Korean?

On its own, nothing: 파르한 spells the sound of Farhan, so the syllables are not Korean words and Koreans read it simply as a foreign name.
