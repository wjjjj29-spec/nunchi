Sakura in Korean 사쿠라

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Sakura as 사쿠라: three syllables, said sa-ku-ra. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Sakura in Hangul
사쿠라
Revised Romanization: sakura · said sa-ku-ra · three syllables

Sakura letter by letter

Pronunciation tip

ㄹ between two vowels (쿠라) is one quick tap of the tongue, like the tt in American "butter": closer to a soft d than an English r.

  • Give all 3 syllables the same weight. Korean has no strong stress, so it's sa-ku-ra, evenly.

How Koreans would call you

Friends calling you over
사쿠라야!
사쿠라 ends in a vowel, so it takes 야. Casual, for close friends.
At work or with new people
사쿠라 씨
사쿠라 ssi. Polite, roughly "Mr" or "Ms", but used with the first name.
Customer, client, online
사쿠라 님
사쿠라 nim. What you'll see on a delivery box or a booking.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables (after a one-syllable family name), so at 3 syllables 사쿠라 reads as a foreign name at a glance. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 사쿠라 or 사쿠라 씨.

Why it's spelled 사쿠라

Here the letters and the sound line up: applying the loanword rules letter by letter already gives 사쿠라, the same spelling Korean media use.

  • Korean writes Japanese names with its own Japanese table: each Japanese syllable maps to one Hangul syllable and long vowels are not marked.

Questions people ask

How do you write Sakura in Korean?

Sakura is written 사쿠라 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: sakura). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name and follows the Japanese pronunciation. In Hangul it has three syllables: 사 + 쿠 + 라.

How do you pronounce Sakura in Korean?

Say it sa-ku-ra, with even weight on every syllable. ㄹ between two vowels (쿠라) is one quick tap of the tongue, like the tt in American "butter": closer to a soft d than an English r.
