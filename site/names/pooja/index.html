Pronunciation tip

ㅍ at the start is aspirated: a clear puff of air, like the p in "pie".

  • Two even beats, pu-ja: Korean doesn't stress one syllable over the other the way English does.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables, so 푸자 already sounds natural to Korean ears. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 푸자 or 푸자 씨.

Why it's spelled 푸자

Here the letters and the sound line up: applying the loanword rules letter by letter already gives 푸자, the same spelling Korean media use.

Questions people ask

How do you write Pooja in Korean?

Pooja is written 푸자 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: puja). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has two syllables: 푸 + 자.

How do you pronounce Pooja in Korean?

Say it pu-ja, with even weight on every syllable. ㅍ at the start is aspirated: a clear puff of air, like the p in "pie".

What does Pooja mean in Korean?

On its own, nothing: 푸자 spells the sound of Pooja, so the syllables are not Korean words and Koreans read it simply as a foreign name.
