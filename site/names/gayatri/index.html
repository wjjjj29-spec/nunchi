Pronunciation tip

ㄹ between two vowels (트리) is one quick tap of the tongue, like the tt in American "butter": closer to a soft d than an English r.

  • 트 carries the filler vowel ㅡ (eu). Keep it short and light, almost just the consonant.
  • ㄱ at the start is soft and unvoiced, halfway between k and g.
  • ㅇ at the top of 야 is silent, so 야 is just the vowel: ya.
  • Give all 4 syllables the same weight. Korean has no strong stress, so it's ga-ya-teu-ri, evenly.

  • Korean adds the vowel ㅡ to consonants that have no vowel of their own (트), which is why Gayatri has 4 syllables in Korean.

Questions people ask

How do you write Gayatri in Korean?

Gayatri is written 가야트리 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: gayateuri). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has four syllables: 가 + 야 + 트 + 리.

How do you pronounce Gayatri in Korean?

Say it ga-ya-teu-ri, with even weight on every syllable. ㄹ between two vowels (트리) is one quick tap of the tongue, like the tt in American "butter": closer to a soft d than an English r.

What does Gayatri mean in Korean?

On its own, nothing: 가야트리 spells the sound of Gayatri, so the syllables are not Korean words and Koreans read it simply as a foreign name.
