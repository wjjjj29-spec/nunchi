Vishal in Korean 비샬

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Vishal as 비샬: two syllables, said bi-syal. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Vishal in Hangul
비샬
Revised Romanization: bisyal · said bi-syal · two syllables

Vishal letter by letter

Each Hangul block is one syllable, built from a consonant, a vowel and sometimes a final consonant underneath.

bi
샬
ㅅ s + ㅑ ya + ㄹ l (final)

Pronunciation tip

ㄹ at the bottom of 샬 is an l: the tongue touches just behind the teeth and stays there.

  • ㅂ at the start is soft and unvoiced, halfway between p and b.
  • Two even beats, bi-syal: Korean doesn't stress one syllable over the other the way English does.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables, so 비샬 already sounds natural to Korean ears. In casual sentences Koreans often add 이 after a name that ends in a consonant: 비샬이 왔어 ("Vishal's here").

Why it's spelled 비샬

Here the letters and the sound line up: applying the loanword rules letter by letter already gives 비샬, the same spelling Korean media use.

  • There is no v in Korean either: v becomes ㅂ (b).

Questions people ask

How do you write Vishal in Korean?

Vishal is written 비샬 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: bisyal). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has two syllables: 비 + 샬.

How do you pronounce Vishal in Korean?

Say it bi-syal, with even weight on every syllable. ㄹ at the bottom of 샬 is an l: the tongue touches just behind the teeth and stays there.

What does Vishal mean in Korean?

On its own, nothing: 비샬 spells the sound of Vishal, so the syllables are not Korean words and Koreans read it simply as a foreign name.
