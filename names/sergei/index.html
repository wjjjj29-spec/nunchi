Sergei in Korean 세르게이

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Sergei as 세르게이: four syllables, said se-reu-ge-i. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Pronunciation tip

ㄹ between two vowels (세르) is one quick tap of the tongue, like the tt in American "butter": closer to a soft d than an English r.

  • 르 carries the filler vowel ㅡ (eu). Keep it short and light, almost just the consonant.
  • ㅇ at the top of 이 is silent, so 이 is just the vowel: i.
  • Give all 4 syllables the same weight. Korean has no strong stress, so it's se-reu-ge-i, evenly.

Customer, client, online
세르게이 님
세르게이 nim. What you'll see on a delivery box or a booking.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables (after a one-syllable family name), so at 4 syllables 세르게이 reads as a foreign name at a glance. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 세르게이 or 세르게이 씨.

Spelled letter-by-letter you'd get 서제이, but Korean media write 세르게이, because the name is written the way it sounds in Russian, not the way it's spelled.

  • The Korean spelling comes from the Russian (Cyrillic) form, so it follows Russian pronunciation rather than the English letters.

Questions people ask

How do you write Sergei in Korean?

Sergei is written 세르게이 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: sereugei). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name and follows the Russian pronunciation. In Hangul it has four syllables: 세 + 르 + 게 + 이.

How do you pronounce Sergei in Korean?

Say it se-reu-ge-i, with even weight on every syllable. ㄹ between two vowels (세르) is one quick tap of the tongue, like the tt in American "butter": closer to a soft d than an English r.
