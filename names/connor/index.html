Connor in Korean 코너

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Connor as 코너: two syllables, said ko-neo. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Pronunciation tip

ㅓ (eo) in 너 is the open "uh" of "sun", not an "o".

  • ㅋ at the start is aspirated: a clear puff of air, like the k in "kite".
  • ㅗ is a pure "o" with rounded lips. Don't add the w-glide English puts in "go".
  • Two even beats, ko-neo: Korean doesn't stress one syllable over the other the way English does.

코너 nim. What you'll see on a delivery box or a booking.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables, so 코너 already sounds natural to Korean ears. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 코너 or 코너 씨.

How Koreans write it
코너

Spelled letter-by-letter you'd get 코노, but Korean media write 코너, because the name is written the way it sounds, not the way it's spelled.

  • An r after a vowel (before a consonant or at the end) isn't written. Korean spelling follows British-style pronunciation, where that r is silent.
  • The unstressed "uh" vowel is written ㅓ (eo), whatever letter English uses for it.
  • Double letters count once. Hangul spells the sound, not the letters.

Questions people ask

How do you write Connor in Korean?

Connor is written 코너 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: koneo). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has two syllables: 코 + 너.

How do you pronounce Connor in Korean?

Say it ko-neo, with even weight on every syllable. ㅓ (eo) in 너 is the open "uh" of "sun", not an "o".
