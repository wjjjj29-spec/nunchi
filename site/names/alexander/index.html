Alexander in Korean 알렉산더

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Alexander as 알렉산더: four syllables, said al-lek-san-deo. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Pronunciation tip

The double ㄹ in 알렉 is a clear English l, as in "hello", not an r.

  • The final ㄱ in 렉 is unreleased: stop the sound (k) without a puff of air.
  • ㅓ (eo) in 더 is the open "uh" of "sun", not an "o".
  • Give all 4 syllables the same weight. Korean has no strong stress, so it's al-lek-san-deo, evenly.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables (after a one-syllable family name), so at 4 syllables 알렉산더 reads as a foreign name at a glance. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 알렉산더 or 알렉산더 씨.

Why it's spelled 알렉산더

How Koreans write it
알렉산더
the settled media spelling

Spelled letter-by-letter you'd get 알레크산더, but Korean media write 알렉산더, because the name is written the way it sounds, not the way it's spelled.

  • x is two sounds, k and s, so Hangul writes both: ㄱ (k) and ㅅ (s).
  • An r after a vowel (before a consonant or at the end) isn't written. Korean spelling follows British-style pronunciation, where that r is silent.
  • An l between vowels is written twice (final ㄹ + ㄹ), so Koreans say a clear l instead of a tapped r.
  • The unstressed "uh" vowel is written ㅓ (eo), whatever letter English uses for it.

Questions people ask

How do you write Alexander in Korean?

Alexander is written 알렉산더 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: alleksandeo). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has four syllables: 알 + 렉 + 산 + 더.

How do you pronounce Alexander in Korean?

Say it al-lek-san-deo, with even weight on every syllable. The double ㄹ in 알렉 is a clear English l, as in "hello", not an r.
