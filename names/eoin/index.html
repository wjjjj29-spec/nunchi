Eoin in Korean 오언

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Eoin as 오언: two syllables, said o-eon. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Pronunciation tip

ㅓ (eo) in 언 is the open "uh" of "sun", not an "o".

  • ㅗ is a pure "o" with rounded lips. Don't add the w-glide English puts in "go".
  • ㅇ at the top of 언 is silent, so 언 is just the vowel: eon.
  • Two even beats, o-eon: Korean doesn't stress one syllable over the other the way English does.

Spelled letter-by-letter you'd get 에오인, but Korean media write 오언, because the name is written the way it sounds in Irish, not the way it's spelled.

  • It is an Irish (Gaelic) name, and Irish spelling hides the sound: several vowels are silent. Korean writes what you hear, not the letters.

Questions people ask

How do you write Eoin in Korean?

Eoin is written 오언 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: oeon). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name and follows the Irish pronunciation. In Hangul it has two syllables: 오 + 언.

How do you pronounce Eoin in Korean?

Say it o-eon, with even weight on every syllable. ㅓ (eo) in 언 is the open "uh" of "sun", not an "o".
