Georgia in Korean 조지아

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Georgia as 조지아: three syllables, said jo-ji-a. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Pronunciation tip

ㅈ at the start is soft and unvoiced, halfway between ch and j.

  • ㅗ is a pure "o" with rounded lips. Don't add the w-glide English puts in "go".
  • ㅇ at the top of 아 is silent, so 아 is just the vowel: a.
  • Give all 3 syllables the same weight. Korean has no strong stress, so it's jo-ji-a, evenly.

At work or with new people
조지아 씨
조지아 ssi. Polite, roughly "Mr" or "Ms", but used with the first name.
Customer, client, online
조지아 님
조지아 nim. What you'll see on a delivery box or a booking.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables (after a one-syllable family name), so at 3 syllables 조지아 reads as a foreign name at a glance. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 조지아 or 조지아 씨.

How Koreans write it

Spelled letter-by-letter you'd get 제오지아, but Korean media write 조지아, because the name is written the way it sounds, not the way it's spelled.

  • An r after a vowel (before a consonant or at the end) isn't written. Korean spelling follows British-style pronunciation, where that r is silent.

Questions people ask

How do you write Georgia in Korean?

Georgia is written 조지아 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: jojia). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has three syllables: 조 + 지 + 아.

How do you pronounce Georgia in Korean?

Say it jo-ji-a, with even weight on every syllable. ㅈ at the start is soft and unvoiced, halfway between ch and j.
