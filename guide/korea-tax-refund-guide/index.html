Korea tax refund: how to get the VAT back in 2026

Spend ₩15,000 at a Tax Free shop and show your passport: the VAT comes off at the till. Bigger buys go through the airport.

4 min readChecked 20 October 2026
At a glance1 / 6
The short version
  • Minimum: ₩15,000 on one receipt at a shop with a Tax Free sign. It was ₩30,000 before 2024, so ignore older guides.
  • At the till: show your passport and the VAT comes off straight away, for purchases under ₩1,000,000, up to ₩5,000,000 per trip.
  • At the airport: scan your slips at a kiosk before check-in, see customs if it flags you, then collect cash or a card refund after security.
  • Expect: about 5–8% back, not 10%, after operator fees. The cosmetic procedure refund ended on 31 December 2025.

Who can claim, and what counts

Foreign visitors staying in Korea for less than six months can get the VAT back on goods bought at shops with a Tax Free sign (사후면세점 sahu myeonsejeom, "tax-refund shop"). Overseas Koreans who have lived abroad for two years or more qualify too. The goods have to leave Korea with you within three months of purchase.

The minimum is ₩15,000 on one receipt at one shop. It was ₩30,000 until the end of 2023, and some older guides and shop signs still say so. Separate receipts can't be added together afterwards, so put everything through in one go. Restaurants, cafés, tours and most hotels don't count; it's for goods you take home.

Instant refund at the till

Most Tax Free shops in tourist areas take the tax off on the spot (즉시환급 jeuksi hwangeup, "immediate refund"). Hand over your passport before the cashier rings things up and you simply pay the lower price. There's nothing to do at the airport afterwards. The limits doubled on 1 January 2024:

RuleSince 2024Before 2024
Minimum per receipt₩15,000₩30,000
Instant refund, per purchaseunder ₩1,000,000under ₩500,000
Instant refund, whole trip₩5,000,000₩2,500,000

Go over either limit, or shop somewhere without instant refund, and you pay the full price and get a refund slip (물품판매확인서, the tax refund receipt) to claim at the airport. An ordinary till receipt isn't enough. Bring your physical passport; cashiers scan it, and a photo on your phone usually won't do.

To ask, say 택스 리펀 돼요? (taekseu ripeon dwaeyo?, "Can I get a tax refund?").

Olive Young, Daiso and department stores

  • Olive Young: tourist-area branches do instant refunds with your passport. A few things are excluded, such as sanitary products and shopping bags.
  • Daiso: many but not all branches are Tax Free shops. The big tourist branches, such as the Myeongdong flagship, are the safest bet. ₩15,000 at Daiso is a big basket, so it's worth asking.
  • Department stores: most have a refund desk. Keep the slips together and ask staff where to take them.

How much you actually get

Korean VAT is 10% of the pre-tax price, which works out at about 9.1% of the price on the tag. Refund operators keep a fee, so most people get back about 5–8% of what they paid, a little more on bigger purchases. Instant refunds and airport refunds both go through these operators, including Global Tax Free, Global Blue and Easy Tax Refund.

Claiming at Incheon Airport

  • Before check-in: keep the goods and slips with you. Scan your passport and slips at a refund kiosk in the departures hall.
  • If the kiosk flags you, take your passport, the slips and the goods (unopened, unused) to the customs desk for an export stamp. Bigger refunds are the ones most likely to be checked.
  • Goods in your checked bag? Tell the airline at check-in. They tag the bag and give it back, you show it at customs, then drop it at the oversized-baggage belt next to customs.
  • After security, collect the money at a refund counter or machine, in cash or to your card.

Allow an extra 30 minutes; the customs queue can be long in the evening. If customs wants to see something that's already in your checked bag, you can lose that refund. Heading out from Seoul? See getting to Incheon to time your trip.

Gimpo, Busan and refunds in the city

Gimpo and Gimhae (Busan) international terminals have customs desks and refund points too. They're smaller than Incheon's, so ask at the information desk if you can't see the signs.

Global Tax Free and Global Blue also run downtown refund points in tourist areas and some department stores, which pay out before you fly. You'll need a credit card as a guarantee, you must leave Korea within the period they give you, and you still scan the slips at the airport. If you don't, the refund can be charged back to that card.

What changed in 2026

The VAT refund on cosmetic and medical procedures for foreign patients ended on 31 December 2025, so clinics no longer give that money back. Shopping refunds are unchanged.

A separate VAT refund on hotel stays of up to 30 nights still exists, but only at hotels the culture ministry designates each quarter (110 in the third quarter of 2026). Ask at check-in whether yours is on the list.

Nunchi tip

Cosmetics bought with an instant refund may come sealed in a Tax Free bag. Leave it closed until you've left Korea. Opening and using the goods here means you're technically liable for the tax.

Questions people ask

What is the minimum purchase for a tax refund in Korea?

₩15,000 on one receipt at a Tax Free shop. The minimum was lowered from ₩30,000 on 1 January 2024. Separate receipts can't be combined later.

What are the instant tax refund limits in Korea in 2026?

Each purchase must be under ₩1,000,000, and your instant-refund purchases can add up to ₩5,000,000 per trip. Above that, you pay full price and claim at the airport.

How much of the price do I get back?

Usually about 5–8%. VAT is 10% of the pre-tax price, about 9.1% of what you pay, and the refund operator keeps a fee.

Do I need to show my purchases at the airport?

Only if the refund kiosk flags them, which usually happens with larger refunds. Keep the goods unopened and with you, not in your checked bag, until you've been through the kiosk and customs.

Can I still get the VAT back on cosmetic surgery in Korea?

No. The VAT refund on cosmetic and medical procedures for foreign patients ended on 31 December 2025.
