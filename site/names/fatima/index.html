Fatima in Korean 파티마

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Fatima as 파티마: three syllables, said pa-ti-ma. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Pronunciation tip

ㅍ at the start is aspirated: a clear puff of air, like the p in "pie".

  • Give all 3 syllables the same weight. Korean has no strong stress, so it's pa-ti-ma, evenly.

How Koreans would call you

Friends calling you over
파티마야!
파티마 ends in a vowel, so it takes 야. Casual, for close friends.
At work or with new people
파티마 씨
파티마 ssi. Polite, roughly "Mr" or "Ms", but used with the first name.
Customer, client, online
파티마 님
파티마 nim. What you'll see on a delivery box or a booking.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables (after a one-syllable family name), so at 3 syllables 파티마 reads as a foreign name at a glance. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 파티마 or 파티마 씨.

Why it's spelled 파티마

Here the letters and the sound line up: applying the loanword rules letter by letter already gives 파티마, the same spelling Korean media use.

  • Arabic names reach Korean through different romanizations, so Korean media spellings can vary; this is the common one.

Questions people ask

How do you write Fatima in Korean?

Fatima is written 파티마 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: patima). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name and follows the Arabic pronunciation. In Hangul it has three syllables: 파 + 티 + 마.

How do you pronounce Fatima in Korean?

Say it pa-ti-ma, with even weight on every syllable. ㅍ at the start is aspirated: a clear puff of air, like the p in "pie".
