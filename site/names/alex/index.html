Alex in Korean 알렉스

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Alex as 알렉스: three syllables, said al-lek-seu. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

알렉스

Pronunciation tip

The double ㄹ in 알렉 is a clear English l, as in "hello", not an r.

  • 스 carries the filler vowel ㅡ (eu). Keep it short and light, almost just the consonant.
  • The final ㄱ in 렉 is unreleased: stop the sound (k) without a puff of air.
  • Give all 3 syllables the same weight. Korean has no strong stress, so it's al-lek-seu, evenly.

알렉스 nim. What you'll see on a delivery box or a booking.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables (after a one-syllable family name), so at 3 syllables 알렉스 reads as a foreign name at a glance. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 알렉스 or 알렉스 씨.

Why it's spelled 알렉스

Letter by letter
앨렉스
what spelling rules alone give
How Koreans write it
알렉스
the settled media spelling

Spelled letter-by-letter you'd get 앨렉스, but Korean media write 알렉스, because the name is written the way it sounds, not the way it's spelled.

  • x is two sounds, k and s, so Hangul writes both: ㄱ (k) and ㅅ (s).
  • An l between vowels is written twice (final ㄹ + ㄹ), so Koreans say a clear l instead of a tapped r.
  • Korean adds the vowel ㅡ to consonants that have no vowel of their own (스), which is why Alex has 3 syllables in Korean.

Questions people ask

How do you write Alex in Korean?

Alex is written 알렉스 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: allekseu). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has three syllables: 알 + 렉 + 스.

How do you pronounce Alex in Korean?

Say it al-lek-seu, with even weight on every syllable. The double ㄹ in 알렉 is a clear English l, as in "hello", not an r.
