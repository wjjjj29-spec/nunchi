Hugo in Korean 위고

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Hugo as 위고: two syllables, said wi-go. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Pronunciation tip

ㅟ in 위 is one gliding vowel: wee, in a single beat.

  • ㅗ is a pure "o" with rounded lips. Don't add the w-glide English puts in "go".
  • Two even beats, wi-go: Korean doesn't stress one syllable over the other the way English does.

Customer, client, online
위고 님
위고 nim. What you'll see on a delivery box or a booking.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables, so 위고 already sounds natural to Korean ears. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 위고 or 위고 씨.

How Koreans write it
위고
the settled media spelling

Spelled letter-by-letter you'd get 휴고, but Korean media write 위고, because the name is written the way it sounds in French, not the way it's spelled.

  • Korean writes French names the French way: the h is silent and u is the French 'ü' sound, written 위.

Questions people ask

How do you write Hugo in Korean?

Hugo is written 위고 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: wigo). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name and follows the French pronunciation. In Hangul it has two syllables: 위 + 고.

How do you pronounce Hugo in Korean?

Say it wi-go, with even weight on every syllable. ㅟ in 위 is one gliding vowel: wee, in a single beat.
