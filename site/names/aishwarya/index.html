Pronunciation tip

ㄹ between two vowels (와리) is one quick tap of the tongue, like the tt in American "butter": closer to a soft d than an English r.

  • ㅘ in 와 is one gliding vowel: wa, in a single beat.
  • ㅇ at the top of 이 is silent, so 이 is just the vowel: i.
  • Give all 6 syllables the same weight. Korean has no strong stress, so it's a-i-syu-wa-ri-a, evenly.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables (after a one-syllable family name), so at 6 syllables 아이슈와리아 reads as a foreign name at a glance. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 아이슈와리아 or 아이슈와리아 씨.

Spelled letter-by-letter you'd get 에이시와야, but Korean media write 아이슈와리아, because the name is written the way it sounds, not the way it's spelled.

  • The long i (as in "my") is spelled 아이: two beats in Korean, one in English.
  • w merges with the vowel after it into one Korean vowel: 와.

Questions people ask

How do you write Aishwarya in Korean?

Aishwarya is written 아이슈와리아 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: aisyuwaria). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has six syllables: 아 + 이 + 슈 + 와 + 리 + 아.

How do you pronounce Aishwarya in Korean?

Say it a-i-syu-wa-ri-a, with even weight on every syllable. ㄹ between two vowels (와리) is one quick tap of the tongue, like the tt in American "butter": closer to a soft d than an English r.

What does Aishwarya mean in Korean?

On its own, nothing: 아이슈와리아 spells the sound of Aishwarya, so the syllables are not Korean words and Koreans read it simply as a foreign name.
