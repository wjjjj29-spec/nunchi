Timothy in Korean 티머시

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Timothy as 티머시: three syllables, said ti-meo-si. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Pronunciation tip

ㅓ (eo) in 머 is the open "uh" of "sun", not an "o".

  • ㅌ at the start is aspirated: a clear puff of air, like the t in "top".
  • Give all 3 syllables the same weight. Korean has no strong stress, so it's ti-meo-si, evenly.

Customer, client, online

티머시 nim. What you'll see on a delivery box or a booking.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables (after a one-syllable family name), so at 3 syllables 티머시 reads as a foreign name at a glance. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 티머시 or 티머시 씨.

Why it's spelled 티머시

How Koreans write it
티머시
the settled media spelling

Spelled letter-by-letter you'd get 티모시, but Korean media write 티머시, because the name is written the way it sounds, not the way it's spelled.

  • Korean has no th, so th becomes ㅅ (s), the closest sound.
  • The unstressed "uh" vowel is written ㅓ (eo), whatever letter English uses for it.

Questions people ask

How do you write Timothy in Korean?

Timothy is written 티머시 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: timeosi). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has three syllables: 티 + 머 + 시.

How do you pronounce Timothy in Korean?

Say it ti-meo-si, with even weight on every syllable. ㅓ (eo) in 머 is the open "uh" of "sun", not an "o".
