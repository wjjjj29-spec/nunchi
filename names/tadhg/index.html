Tadhg in Korean 타이그

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Tadhg as 타이그: three syllables, said ta-i-geu. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Pronunciation tip

그 carries the filler vowel ㅡ (eu). Keep it short and light, almost just the consonant.

  • ㅌ at the start is aspirated: a clear puff of air, like the t in "top".
  • ㅇ at the top of 이 is silent, so 이 is just the vowel: i.
  • Give all 3 syllables the same weight. Korean has no strong stress, so it's ta-i-geu, evenly.

Customer, client, online
타이그 님
타이그 nim. What you'll see on a delivery box or a booking.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables (after a one-syllable family name), so at 3 syllables 타이그 reads as a foreign name at a glance. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 타이그 or 타이그 씨.

How Koreans write it

Spelled letter-by-letter you'd get 태드흐그, but Korean media write 타이그, because the name is written the way it sounds in Irish, not the way it's spelled.

  • It is an Irish (Gaelic) name, and Irish spelling hides the sound: dh is silent and several vowels are silent. Korean writes what you hear, not the letters.

Questions people ask

How do you write Tadhg in Korean?

Tadhg is written 타이그 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: taigeu). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name and follows the Irish pronunciation. In Hangul it has three syllables: 타 + 이 + 그.

How do you pronounce Tadhg in Korean?

Say it ta-i-geu, with even weight on every syllable. 그 carries the filler vowel ㅡ (eu). Keep it short and light, almost just the consonant.
