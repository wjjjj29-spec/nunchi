Teddy in Korean 테디

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Teddy as 테디: two syllables, said te-di. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Teddy in Hangul
테디
Revised Romanization: tedi · said te-di · two syllables

Teddy letter by letter

Each Hangul block is one syllable, built from a consonant, a vowel and sometimes a final consonant underneath.

테
ㅌ t + ㅔ e
te
디
ㄷ d + ㅣ i
di
Pronunciation tip

ㅌ at the start is aspirated: a clear puff of air, like the t in "top".

  • Two even beats, te-di: Korean doesn't stress one syllable over the other the way English does.

How Koreans would call you

Friends calling you over
테디야!
테디 ends in a vowel, so it takes 야. Casual, for close friends.
At work or with new people
테디 씨
테디 ssi. Polite, roughly "Mr" or "Ms", but used with the first name.
Customer, client, online
테디 님
테디 nim. What you'll see on a delivery box or a booking.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables, so 테디 already sounds natural to Korean ears. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 테디 or 테디 씨.

Why it's spelled 테디

Here the letters and the sound line up: applying the loanword rules letter by letter already gives 테디, the same spelling Korean media use.

  • Double letters count once. Hangul spells the sound, not the letters.

Questions people ask

How do you write Teddy in Korean?

Teddy is written 테디 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: tedi). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has two syllables: 테 + 디.

How do you pronounce Teddy in Korean?

Say it te-di, with even weight on every syllable. ㅌ at the start is aspirated: a clear puff of air, like the t in "top".
