Ava in Korean 에이바

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Ava as 에이바: three syllables, said e-i-ba. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Pronunciation tip

ㅇ at the top of 이 is silent, so 이 is just the vowel: i.

  • Give all 3 syllables the same weight. Korean has no strong stress, so it's e-i-ba, evenly.

How Koreans would call you

Friends calling you over
에이바야!
에이바 ends in a vowel, so it takes 야. Casual, for close friends.
At work or with new people
에이바 씨
에이바 ssi. Polite, roughly "Mr" or "Ms", but used with the first name.
Customer, client, online
에이바 님
에이바 nim. What you'll see on a delivery box or a booking.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables (after a one-syllable family name), so at 3 syllables 에이바 reads as a foreign name at a glance. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 에이바 or 에이바 씨.

Why it's spelled 에이바

How Koreans write it
에이바
the settled media spelling

Spelled letter-by-letter you'd get 애바, but Korean media write 에이바, because the name is written the way it sounds, not the way it's spelled.

  • There is no v in Korean either: v becomes ㅂ (b).
  • The long a (as in "day") is spelled as two beats, 에이, because Korean writes each part of the diphthong.

Questions people ask

How do you write Ava in Korean?

Ava is written 에이바 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: eiba). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has three syllables: 에 + 이 + 바.

How do you pronounce Ava in Korean?

Say it e-i-ba, with even weight on every syllable. ㅇ at the top of 이 is silent, so 이 is just the vowel: i.
