Tom in Korean 톰

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Tom as 톰: one syllable, said tom. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Tom in Hangul
톰
Revised Romanization: tom · said tom · one syllable

Tom letter by letter

Each Hangul block is one syllable, built from a consonant, a vowel and sometimes a final consonant underneath.

톰
ㅌ t + ㅗ o + ㅁ m (final)
tom
Pronunciation tip

ㅌ at the start is aspirated: a clear puff of air, like the t in "top".

  • ㅗ is a pure "o" with rounded lips. Don't add the w-glide English puts in "go".
  • It's a single syllable in Korean, so say it in one short beat: tom.

How Koreans would call you

Friends calling you over
톰아!
톰 ends in a consonant, so it takes 아, said 토마. Casual, for close friends.
At work or with new people
톰 씨
톰 ssi. Polite, roughly "Mr" or "Ms", but used with the first name.
Customer, client, online
톰 님
톰 nim. What you'll see on a delivery box or a booking.

One syllable is short for Korea, where given names are almost always two, so 톰 stands out and is easy to remember. In casual sentences Koreans often add 이 after a name that ends in a consonant: 톰이 왔어 ("Tom's here").

Why it's spelled 톰

Here the letters and the sound line up: applying the loanword rules letter by letter already gives 톰, the same spelling Korean media use.
