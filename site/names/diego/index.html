Diego in Korean 디에고

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Diego as 디에고: three syllables, said di-e-go. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Pronunciation tip

ㄷ at the start is soft and unvoiced, halfway between t and d.

  • ㅗ is a pure "o" with rounded lips. Don't add the w-glide English puts in "go".
  • ㅇ at the top of 에 is silent, so 에 is just the vowel: e.
  • Give all 3 syllables the same weight. Korean has no strong stress, so it's di-e-go, evenly.

How Koreans would call you

Friends calling you over
디에고야!
디에고 ends in a vowel, so it takes 야. Casual, for close friends.
At work or with new people
디에고 씨
디에고 ssi. Polite, roughly "Mr" or "Ms", but used with the first name.
Customer, client, online
디에고 님
디에고 nim. What you'll see on a delivery box or a booking.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables (after a one-syllable family name), so at 3 syllables 디에고 reads as a foreign name at a glance. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 디에고 or 디에고 씨.

Spelled letter-by-letter you'd get 디고, but Korean media write 디에고, because the name is written the way it sounds in Spanish, not the way it's spelled.

  • Korean writes Spanish names by their Spanish sound: vowels are read as written.

Questions people ask

How do you write Diego in Korean?

Diego is written 디에고 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: diego). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name and follows the Spanish pronunciation. In Hangul it has three syllables: 디 + 에 + 고.

How do you pronounce Diego in Korean?

Say it di-e-go, with even weight on every syllable. ㄷ at the start is soft and unvoiced, halfway between t and d.
