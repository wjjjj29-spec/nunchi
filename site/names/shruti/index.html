Pronunciation tip

ㄹ between two vowels (슈루) is one quick tap of the tongue, like the tt in American "butter": closer to a soft d than an English r.

  • Give all 3 syllables the same weight. Korean has no strong stress, so it's syu-ru-ti, evenly.

Questions people ask

How do you write Shruti in Korean?

Shruti is written 슈루티 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: syuruti). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has three syllables: 슈 + 루 + 티.

How do you pronounce Shruti in Korean?

Say it syu-ru-ti, with even weight on every syllable. ㄹ between two vowels (슈루) is one quick tap of the tongue, like the tt in American "butter": closer to a soft d than an English r.

What does Shruti mean in Korean?

On its own, nothing: 슈루티 spells the sound of Shruti, so the syllables are not Korean words and Koreans read it simply as a foreign name.
