James in Korean 제임스

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write James as 제임스: three syllables, said je-im-seu. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Pronunciation tip

스 carries the filler vowel ㅡ (eu). Keep it short and light, almost just the consonant.

  • ㅈ at the start is soft and unvoiced, halfway between ch and j.
  • ㅇ at the top of 임 is silent, so 임 is just the vowel: im.
  • Give all 3 syllables the same weight. Korean has no strong stress, so it's je-im-seu, evenly.

How Koreans would call you

Friends calling you over
제임스야!
제임스 ends in a vowel, so it takes 야. Casual, for close friends.
At work or with new people
제임스 씨
제임스 ssi. Polite, roughly "Mr" or "Ms", but used with the first name.
Customer, client, online
제임스 님
제임스 nim. What you'll see on a delivery box or a booking.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables (after a one-syllable family name), so at 3 syllables 제임스 reads as a foreign name at a glance. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 제임스 or 제임스 씨.

Spelled letter-by-letter you'd get 재메스, but Korean media write 제임스, because the name is written the way it sounds, not the way it's spelled.

  • Korean adds the vowel ㅡ to consonants that have no vowel of their own (스), which is why James has 3 syllables in Korean.

Questions people ask

How do you write James in Korean?

James is written 제임스 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: jeimseu). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has three syllables: 제 + 임 + 스.

How do you pronounce James in Korean?

Say it je-im-seu, with even weight on every syllable. 스 carries the filler vowel ㅡ (eu). Keep it short and light, almost just the consonant.
