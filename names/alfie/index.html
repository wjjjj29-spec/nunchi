Pronunciation tip

ㄹ at the bottom of 앨 is an l: the tongue touches just behind the teeth and stays there.

  • ㅐ (ae) is said like the e in "bed" by most Koreans today, a little more closed than the a in "cat".
  • Two even beats, ael-pi: Korean doesn't stress one syllable over the other the way English does.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables, so 앨피 already sounds natural to Korean ears. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 앨피 or 앨피 씨.

Why it's spelled 앨피

Here the letters and the sound line up: applying the loanword rules letter by letter already gives 앨피, the same spelling Korean media use.

  • Korean has no f sound, so f becomes ㅍ (p), said with a puff of air.

Questions people ask

How do you write Alfie in Korean?

Alfie is written 앨피 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: aelpi). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has two syllables: 앨 + 피.

How do you pronounce Alfie in Korean?

Say it ael-pi, with even weight on every syllable. ㄹ at the bottom of 앨 is an l: the tongue touches just behind the teeth and stays there.
