Poppy in Korean 포피

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Poppy as 포피: two syllables, said po-pi. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Pronunciation tip

ㅍ at the start is aspirated: a clear puff of air, like the p in "pie".

  • ㅗ is a pure "o" with rounded lips. Don't add the w-glide English puts in "go".
  • Two even beats, po-pi: Korean doesn't stress one syllable over the other the way English does.

How Koreans would call you

Friends calling you over
포피야!
포피 ends in a vowel, so it takes 야. Casual, for close friends.
At work or with new people
포피 씨
포피 ssi. Polite, roughly "Mr" or "Ms", but used with the first name.
Customer, client, online
포피 님
포피 nim. What you'll see on a delivery box or a booking.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables, so 포피 already sounds natural to Korean ears. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 포피 or 포피 씨.

Why it's spelled 포피

Here the letters and the sound line up: applying the loanword rules letter by letter already gives 포피, the same spelling Korean media use.

  • Double letters count once. Hangul spells the sound, not the letters.

Questions people ask

How do you write Poppy in Korean?

Poppy is written 포피 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: popi). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has two syllables: 포 + 피.

How do you pronounce Poppy in Korean?

Say it po-pi, with even weight on every syllable. ㅍ at the start is aspirated: a clear puff of air, like the p in "pie".
