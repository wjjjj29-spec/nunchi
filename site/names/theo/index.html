Theo in Korean 시오

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Theo as 시오: two syllables, said si-o. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Theo letter by letter

Each Hangul block is one syllable, built from a consonant, a vowel and sometimes a final consonant underneath.

Pronunciation tip

ㅗ is a pure "o" with rounded lips. Don't add the w-glide English puts in "go".

  • ㅇ at the top of 오 is silent, so 오 is just the vowel: o.
  • Two even beats, si-o: Korean doesn't stress one syllable over the other the way English does.

How Koreans would call you

Friends calling you over
시오야!
시오 ends in a vowel, so it takes 야. Casual, for close friends.
At work or with new people
시오 씨
시오 ssi. Polite, roughly "Mr" or "Ms", but used with the first name.
Customer, client, online
시오 님
시오 nim. What you'll see on a delivery box or a booking.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables, so 시오 already sounds natural to Korean ears. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 시오 or 시오 씨.

Spelled letter-by-letter you'd get 세오, but Korean media write 시오, because the name is written the way it sounds, not the way it's spelled.

  • Korean has no th, so th becomes ㅅ (s), the closest sound.

Questions people ask

How do you write Theo in Korean?

Theo is written 시오 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: sio). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has two syllables: 시 + 오.

How do you pronounce Theo in Korean?

Say it si-o, with even weight on every syllable. ㅗ is a pure "o" with rounded lips. Don't add the w-glide English puts in "go".
