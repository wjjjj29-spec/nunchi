Niamh in Korean 니브

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Niamh as 니브: two syllables, said ni-beu. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Niamh letter by letter

Each Hangul block is one syllable, built from a consonant, a vowel and sometimes a final consonant underneath.

Pronunciation tip

브 carries the filler vowel ㅡ (eu). Keep it short and light, almost just the consonant.

  • Two even beats, ni-beu: Korean doesn't stress one syllable over the other the way English does.

How Koreans would call you

니브 ends in a vowel, so it takes 야. Casual, for close friends.
At work or with new people

니브 ssi. Polite, roughly "Mr" or "Ms", but used with the first name.
Customer, client, online
니브 님
니브 nim. What you'll see on a delivery box or a booking.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables, so 니브 already sounds natural to Korean ears. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 니브 or 니브 씨.

Why it's spelled 니브

How Koreans write it
니브
the settled media spelling

Spelled letter-by-letter you'd get 니앰흐, but Korean media write 니브, because the name is written the way it sounds in Irish, not the way it's spelled.

  • It is an Irish (Gaelic) name, and Irish spelling hides the sound: mh is a v sound and several vowels are silent. Korean writes what you hear, not the letters.

Questions people ask

How do you write Niamh in Korean?

Niamh is written 니브 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: nibeu). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name and follows the Irish pronunciation. In Hangul it has two syllables: 니 + 브.

How do you pronounce Niamh in Korean?

Say it ni-beu, with even weight on every syllable. 브 carries the filler vowel ㅡ (eu). Keep it short and light, almost just the consonant.
