Ivan in Korean 이반

Korean given names are almost always two syllables, so 이반 already sounds natural to Korean ears. In casual sentences Koreans often add 이 after a name that ends in a consonant: 이반이 왔어 ("Ivan's here").

Spelled letter-by-letter you'd get 이밴, but Korean media write 이반, because the name is written the way it sounds in Russian, not the way it's spelled.

  • The Korean spelling comes from the Russian (Cyrillic) form, so it follows Russian pronunciation rather than the English letters.

Questions people ask

How do you write Ivan in Korean?

Ivan is written 이반 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: iban). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name and follows the Russian pronunciation. In Hangul it has two syllables: 이 + 반.

How do you pronounce Ivan in Korean?

Say it i-ban, with even weight on every syllable. Two even beats, i-ban: Korean doesn't stress one syllable over the other the way English does.
