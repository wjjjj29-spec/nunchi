Before you go

Most locations are ordinary streets, cafés and parks where people live and work. Take the photo, buy the coffee, and keep your voice down in residential alleys; Bukchon and Ikseon-dong residents have put up signs for a reason. Cafés that appeared on screen usually have a small "as seen in" card by the till and will not mind you asking which seat it was.

Palaces and parks are free or a few thousand won. Nami Island and Petite France (Winter Sonata, Secret Garden) charge admission and are a day trip from Seoul, easiest by the ITX train to Gapyeong plus a shuttle.

Questions people ask

Are these the real filming spots?

Yes, cross-checked with production credits and the Korea Tourism Organization's drama location listings. Interiors are often sets; we list the exteriors and public places you can actually visit.

Which map app should I use?

Naver Map or Kakao Map for walking and transit directions in Korea; Google Maps works for finding the pin but not for navigation. Both links are provided.
