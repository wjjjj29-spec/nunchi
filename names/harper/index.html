Harper in Korean 하퍼

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Harper as 하퍼: two syllables, said ha-peo. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Pronunciation tip

ㅓ (eo) in 퍼 is the open "uh" of "sun", not an "o".

  • Two even beats, ha-peo: Korean doesn't stress one syllable over the other the way English does.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables, so 하퍼 already sounds natural to Korean ears. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 하퍼 or 하퍼 씨.

Why it's spelled 하퍼

Here the letters and the sound line up: applying the loanword rules letter by letter already gives 하퍼, the same spelling Korean media use.

  • An r after a vowel (before a consonant or at the end) isn't written. Korean spelling follows British-style pronunciation, where that r is silent.
  • The unstressed "uh" vowel is written ㅓ (eo), whatever letter English uses for it.

Questions people ask

How do you write Harper in Korean?

Harper is written 하퍼 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: hapeo). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has two syllables: 하 + 퍼.

How do you pronounce Harper in Korean?

Say it ha-peo, with even weight on every syllable. ㅓ (eo) in 퍼 is the open "uh" of "sun", not an "o".
