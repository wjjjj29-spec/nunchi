Sian in Korean 샨

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Sian as 샨: one syllable, said syan. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

샨 nim. What you'll see on a delivery box or a booking.

One syllable is short for Korea, where given names are almost always two, so 샨 stands out and is easy to remember. In casual sentences Koreans often add 이 after a name that ends in a consonant: 샨이 왔어 ("Sian's here").

How Koreans write it
샨

Spelled letter-by-letter you'd get 시앤, but Korean media write 샨, because the name is written the way it sounds in Welsh, not the way it's spelled.

  • It is a Welsh name, and Welsh spelling has its own rules: si before a vowel is a sh sound. Korean writes the Welsh sound.
