Bayu in Korean 바유

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Bayu as 바유: two syllables, said ba-yu. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Bayu letter by letter

Each Hangul block is one syllable, built from a consonant, a vowel and sometimes a final consonant underneath.

Pronunciation tip

ㅂ at the start is soft and unvoiced, halfway between p and b.

  • ㅇ at the top of 유 is silent, so 유 is just the vowel: yu.
  • Two even beats, ba-yu: Korean doesn't stress one syllable over the other the way English does.

바유 nim. What you'll see on a delivery box or a booking.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables, so 바유 already sounds natural to Korean ears. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 바유 or 바유 씨.

Questions people ask

How do you write Bayu in Korean?

Bayu is written 바유 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: bayu). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has two syllables: 바 + 유.

How do you pronounce Bayu in Korean?

Say it ba-yu, with even weight on every syllable. ㅂ at the start is soft and unvoiced, halfway between p and b.

What does Bayu mean in Korean?

On its own, nothing: 바유 spells the sound of Bayu, so the syllables are not Korean words and Koreans read it simply as a foreign name.
