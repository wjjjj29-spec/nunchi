Esmé in Korean 에스미

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Esmé as 에스미: three syllables, said e-seu-mi. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Pronunciation tip

스 carries the filler vowel ㅡ (eu). Keep it short and light, almost just the consonant.

  • Give all 3 syllables the same weight. Korean has no strong stress, so it's e-seu-mi, evenly.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables (after a one-syllable family name), so at 3 syllables 에스미 reads as a foreign name at a glance. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 에스미 or 에스미 씨.

Spelled letter-by-letter you'd get 에스메, but Korean media write 에스미, because the name is written the way it sounds, not the way it's spelled.

  • Korean adds the vowel ㅡ to consonants that have no vowel of their own (스), which is why Esmé has 3 syllables in Korean.

Questions people ask

How do you write Esmé in Korean?

Esmé is written 에스미 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: eseumi). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has three syllables: 에 + 스 + 미.

How do you pronounce Esmé in Korean?

Say it e-seu-mi, with even weight on every syllable. 스 carries the filler vowel ㅡ (eu). Keep it short and light, almost just the consonant.
