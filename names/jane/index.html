Jane in Korean 제인

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Jane as 제인: two syllables, said je-in. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Jane in Hangul
제인
Revised Romanization: jein · said je-in · two syllables

Jane letter by letter

Each Hangul block is one syllable, built from a consonant, a vowel and sometimes a final consonant underneath.

제
ㅈ j + ㅔ e
je
인
ㅇ silent + ㅣ i + ㄴ n (final)
in
Pronunciation tip

ㅈ at the start is soft and unvoiced, halfway between ch and j.

  • ㅇ at the top of 인 is silent, so 인 is just the vowel: in.
  • Two even beats, je-in: Korean doesn't stress one syllable over the other the way English does.

How Koreans would call you

Friends calling you over
제인아!
제인 ends in a consonant, so it takes 아, said 제이나. Casual, for close friends.
At work or with new people
제인 씨
제인 ssi. Polite, roughly "Mr" or "Ms", but used with the first name.
Customer, client, online
제인 님
제인 nim. What you'll see on a delivery box or a booking.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables, so 제인 already sounds natural to Korean ears. In casual sentences Koreans often add 이 after a name that ends in a consonant: 제인이 왔어 ("Jane's here").

Why it's spelled 제인

Here the letters and the sound line up: applying the loanword rules letter by letter already gives 제인, the same spelling Korean media use.

  • The final e is silent. It only makes the vowel before it long.

Questions people ask

How do you write Jane in Korean?

Jane is written 제인 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: jein). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has two syllables: 제 + 인.

How do you pronounce Jane in Korean?

Say it je-in, with even weight on every syllable. ㅈ at the start is soft and unvoiced, halfway between ch and j.
