Heather in Korean 헤더

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Heather as 헤더: two syllables, said he-deo. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Pronunciation tip

ㅓ (eo) in 더 is the open "uh" of "sun", not an "o".

  • Two even beats, he-deo: Korean doesn't stress one syllable over the other the way English does.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables, so 헤더 already sounds natural to Korean ears. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 헤더 or 헤더 씨.

Why it's spelled 헤더

How Koreans write it
헤더
the settled media spelling

Spelled letter-by-letter you'd get 히서, but Korean media write 헤더, because the name is written the way it sounds, not the way it's spelled.

  • The soft th (as in "the") has no Korean match, so it becomes ㄷ (d).
  • An r after a vowel (before a consonant or at the end) isn't written. Korean spelling follows British-style pronunciation, where that r is silent.
  • The unstressed "uh" vowel is written ㅓ (eo), whatever letter English uses for it.

Questions people ask

How do you write Heather in Korean?

Heather is written 헤더 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: hedeo). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has two syllables: 헤 + 더.

How do you pronounce Heather in Korean?

Say it he-deo, with even weight on every syllable. ㅓ (eo) in 더 is the open "uh" of "sun", not an "o".
