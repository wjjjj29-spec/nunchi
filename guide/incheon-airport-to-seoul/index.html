Incheon Airport to Seoul: train, bus or taxi

Four ways into the city, from ₩4,750 to ₩95,000. Which one is right depends on three things: your bags, your hotel and the time you land.

3 min readChecked 11 October 2026
At a glance1 / 6
The short version
  • Cheapest: AREX all-stop train, ₩4,750 from Terminal 1 to Seoul Station, about an hour. Pay with T-money.
  • Fastest: AREX Express, ₩13,000, 43 minutes non-stop to Seoul Station.
  • Easiest with luggage: limousine bus, ₩17,000–18,000, stops near major hotels.
  • Late or in a group of four: taxi, about ₩70,000–95,000. Fixed fares include tolls.

AREX all-stop train: the cheap one

The airport railroad (AREX) runs from both terminals into the city, stopping at Gimpo Airport, Digital Media City and Hongik University (Hongdae) on the way to Seoul Station. It costs ₩4,750 from Terminal 1 or ₩5,350 from Terminal 2, and takes about an hour.

Pay by tapping a T-money card. Buy one at any convenience store in the arrivals hall and top it up with cash. Foreign credit cards can't be tapped at the gates. The last trains to Seoul Station leave Terminal 1 at 11:38pm and Terminal 2 at 11:32pm.

Seoul's Climate Card won't get you through the gate at the airport stations, so use T-money for this ride.

AREX Express: the fast one

The Express runs non-stop from Terminal 2, via Terminal 1, to Seoul Station: 43 minutes from Terminal 1 for ₩13,000. Every seat is reserved and there's luggage space. Buy a QR ticket at the Express counter on the Transportation Center's B1 level, from a machine, or on the AREX website. Some booking apps sell it slightly cheaper.

It only makes sense if your hotel is near Seoul Station, or if you're changing to a KTX train there. The last Express leaves Terminal 1 at 10:48pm.

Nunchi tip

Keep your Express ticket in mind for the flight home. Express passengers can use the City Airport Terminal at Seoul Station to check in, drop bags and clear immigration before riding to the airport hands-free.

Limousine bus: easiest with luggage

Airport limousine buses go to almost every part of Seoul, and many stop outside big hotels. That means no subway stairs with a 25 kg suitcase. Routes 6001 and 6015 to Myeongdong cost ₩17,000, and most Seoul routes cost ₩17,000–18,000. Allow 60–90 minutes depending on traffic.

Buy a ticket at the booth or kiosk in arrivals. At Terminal 1, a few routes also take T-money when you board. At Terminal 2, every route needs a ticket. The Climate Card isn't accepted.

Taxi: for groups and late arrivals

Split between four people, a taxi costs about the same as four bus tickets and takes you to the door. Regular metered taxis to Myeongdong or Gangnam come to roughly ₩70,000–85,000 including tolls. Between 10pm and 4am the meter runs 20% higher, and 40% higher from 11pm to 2am.

International taxis charge fixed fares, tolls included: ₩85,000 to Jung-gu, Jongno and Yongsan, and ₩90,000 to Gangnam and Seocho (more for a larger van). Book them a day ahead. Only take a taxi from the official rank outside arrivals, never from someone who approaches you inside.

Landing after 11pm

The trains stop around 11:30pm. After that, night buses leave from Terminal 1: N6000 to Gangnam Express Bus Terminal (₩17,000), N6002 towards Hongdae and Cheongnyangni (₩18,000) and N6701 to Dongdaemun Design Plaza (₩18,000). Otherwise, take a taxi and budget for the night rate.

Questions people ask

How much is the AREX Express in 2026?

₩13,000 for an adult from either terminal to Seoul Station. Many older guides still say ₩9,500 or ₩11,000; those fares are out of date.

Can I use a foreign credit card on the airport train?

Not at the gates. Buy a T-money card at an airport convenience store and top it up with cash. You can buy AREX Express tickets with a foreign card at the counter or online.

Does the Climate Card work from Incheon Airport?

No. You can't enter the airport stations with it. Use T-money for the ride in, then switch to the Climate Card in Seoul if it suits your trip.

What if I land after midnight?

Take a night bus (N6000, N6002 or N6701, from ₩17,000) from Terminal 1, or a taxi with the late-night surcharge.
