Bailey in Korean 베일리

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Bailey as 베일리: three syllables, said be-il-li. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Pronunciation tip

The double ㄹ in 일리 is a clear English l, as in "hello", not an r.

  • ㅂ at the start is soft and unvoiced, halfway between p and b.
  • ㅇ at the top of 일 is silent, so 일 is just the vowel: il.
  • Give all 3 syllables the same weight. Korean has no strong stress, so it's be-il-li, evenly.

How Koreans would call you

Friends calling you over
베일리야!
베일리 ends in a vowel, so it takes 야. Casual, for close friends.
At work or with new people
베일리 씨
베일리 ssi. Polite, roughly "Mr" or "Ms", but used with the first name.
Customer, client, online
베일리 님
베일리 nim. What you'll see on a delivery box or a booking.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables (after a one-syllable family name), so at 3 syllables 베일리 reads as a foreign name at a glance. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 베일리 or 베일리 씨.

Why it's spelled 베일리

Here the letters and the sound line up: applying the loanword rules letter by letter already gives 베일리, the same spelling Korean media use.

  • An l between vowels is written twice (final ㄹ + ㄹ), so Koreans say a clear l instead of a tapped r.

Questions people ask

How do you write Bailey in Korean?

How do you pronounce Bailey in Korean?

Say it be-il-li, with even weight on every syllable. The double ㄹ in 일리 is a clear English l, as in "hello", not an r.
