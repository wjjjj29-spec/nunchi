Terry in Korean 테리

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Terry as 테리: two syllables, said te-ri. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Terry letter by letter

Each Hangul block is one syllable, built from a consonant, a vowel and sometimes a final consonant underneath.

Pronunciation tip

ㄹ between two vowels (테리) is one quick tap of the tongue, like the tt in American "butter": closer to a soft d than an English r.

  • ㅌ at the start is aspirated: a clear puff of air, like the t in "top".
  • Two even beats, te-ri: Korean doesn't stress one syllable over the other the way English does.

How Koreans would call you

Friends calling you over
테리야!
테리 ends in a vowel, so it takes 야. Casual, for close friends.
At work or with new people
테리 씨
테리 ssi. Polite, roughly "Mr" or "Ms", but used with the first name.
Customer, client, online
테리 님
테리 nim. What you'll see on a delivery box or a booking.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables, so 테리 already sounds natural to Korean ears. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 테리 or 테리 씨.

Why it's spelled 테리

Here the letters and the sound line up: applying the loanword rules letter by letter already gives 테리, the same spelling Korean media use.

  • Double letters count once. Hangul spells the sound, not the letters.

Questions people ask

How do you write Terry in Korean?

Terry is written 테리 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: teri). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has two syllables: 테 + 리.

How do you pronounce Terry in Korean?

Say it te-ri, with even weight on every syllable. ㄹ between two vowels (테리) is one quick tap of the tongue, like the tt in American "butter": closer to a soft d than an English r.
