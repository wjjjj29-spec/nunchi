Chloe in Korean 클로이

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Chloe as 클로이: three syllables, said keul-lo-i. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Pronunciation tip

The double ㄹ in 클로 is a clear English l, as in "hello", not an r.

  • 클 carries the filler vowel ㅡ (eu). Keep it short and light, almost just the consonant.
  • ㅋ at the start is aspirated: a clear puff of air, like the k in "kite".
  • ㅗ is a pure "o" with rounded lips. Don't add the w-glide English puts in "go".
  • ㅇ at the top of 이 is silent, so 이 is just the vowel: i.

Customer, client, online
클로이 님
클로이 nim. What you'll see on a delivery box or a booking.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables (after a one-syllable family name), so at 3 syllables 클로이 reads as a foreign name at a glance. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 클로이 or 클로이 씨.

Why it's spelled 클로이

How Koreans write it
클로이
the settled media spelling

Spelled letter-by-letter you'd get 크로이, but Korean media write 클로이, because the name is written the way it sounds, not the way it's spelled.

  • The ch in Chloe is a k sound, so Korean uses ㅋ (k), not ㅊ.
  • An l between vowels is written twice (final ㄹ + ㄹ), so Koreans say a clear l instead of a tapped r.
  • Korean adds the vowel ㅡ to consonants that have no vowel of their own (클), which is why Chloe has 3 syllables in Korean.

Questions people ask

How do you write Chloe in Korean?

Chloe is written 클로이 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: keulloi). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has three syllables: 클 + 로 + 이.

How do you pronounce Chloe in Korean?

Say it keul-lo-i, with even weight on every syllable. The double ㄹ in 클로 is a clear English l, as in "hello", not an r.
