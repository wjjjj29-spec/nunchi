Ashley in Korean 애슐리

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Ashley as 애슐리: three syllables, said ae-syul-li. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Pronunciation tip

The double ㄹ in 슐리 is a clear English l, as in "hello", not an r.

  • ㅐ (ae) is said like the e in "bed" by most Koreans today, a little more closed than the a in "cat".
  • Give all 3 syllables the same weight. Korean has no strong stress, so it's ae-syul-li, evenly.

애슐리 nim. What you'll see on a delivery box or a booking.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables (after a one-syllable family name), so at 3 syllables 애슐리 reads as a foreign name at a glance. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 애슐리 or 애슐리 씨.

How Koreans write it
애슐리
the settled media spelling

Spelled letter-by-letter you'd get 애시리, but Korean media write 애슐리, because the name is written the way it sounds, not the way it's spelled.

  • An l between vowels is written twice (final ㄹ + ㄹ), so Koreans say a clear l instead of a tapped r.

Questions people ask

How do you write Ashley in Korean?

Ashley is written 애슐리 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: aesyulli). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has three syllables: 애 + 슐 + 리.

How do you pronounce Ashley in Korean?

Say it ae-syul-li, with even weight on every syllable. The double ㄹ in 슐리 is a clear English l, as in "hello", not an r.
