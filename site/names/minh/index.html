Minh in Korean 민

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Minh as 민: one syllable, said min. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Minh in Hangul
민

Minh letter by letter

Each Hangul block is one syllable, built from a consonant, a vowel and sometimes a final consonant underneath.

민
ㅁ m + ㅣ i + ㄴ n (final)
min
Pronunciation tip

It's a single syllable in Korean, so say it in one short beat: min.

How Koreans would call you

Friends calling you over
민아!
민 ends in a consonant, so it takes 아, said 미나. Casual, for close friends.
At work or with new people
민 씨
민 ssi. Polite, roughly "Mr" or "Ms", but used with the first name.
Customer, client, online
민 님
민 nim. What you'll see on a delivery box or a booking.

One syllable is short for Korea, where given names are almost always two, so 민 stands out and is easy to remember. In casual sentences Koreans often add 이 after a name that ends in a consonant: 민이 왔어 ("Minh's here").

Good to know

민 is also a Korean family name and a very common syllable in Korean given names (민준, 민지).

How Koreans write it
민
the settled media spelling

Spelled letter-by-letter you'd get 민흐, but Korean media write 민, because the name is written the way it sounds in Vietnamese, not the way it's spelled.

  • Korean has an official Vietnamese table: nh, ng and the vowel marks map to specific Hangul, which is why the spelling looks unexpected.
