Sunil in Korean 수닐

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Sunil as 수닐: two syllables, said su-nil. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Sunil letter by letter

Each Hangul block is one syllable, built from a consonant, a vowel and sometimes a final consonant underneath.

Pronunciation tip

ㄹ at the bottom of 닐 is an l: the tongue touches just behind the teeth and stays there.

  • Two even beats, su-nil: Korean doesn't stress one syllable over the other the way English does.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables, so 수닐 already sounds natural to Korean ears. In casual sentences Koreans often add 이 after a name that ends in a consonant: 수닐이 왔어 ("Sunil's here").

Questions people ask

How do you write Sunil in Korean?

Sunil is written 수닐 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: sunil). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has two syllables: 수 + 닐.

How do you pronounce Sunil in Korean?

Say it su-nil, with even weight on every syllable. ㄹ at the bottom of 닐 is an l: the tongue touches just behind the teeth and stays there.

What does Sunil mean in Korean?

On its own, nothing: 수닐 spells the sound of Sunil, so the syllables are not Korean words and Koreans read it simply as a foreign name.
