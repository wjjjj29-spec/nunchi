Riley in Korean 라일리

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Riley as 라일리: three syllables, said ra-il-li. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Pronunciation tip

The opening ㄹ is a single light tap, halfway between an English r and l. Never roll it.

  • The double ㄹ in 일리 is a clear English l, as in "hello", not an r.
  • ㅇ at the top of 일 is silent, so 일 is just the vowel: il.
  • Give all 3 syllables the same weight. Korean has no strong stress, so it's ra-il-li, evenly.

How Koreans would call you

Friends calling you over
라일리야!
라일리 ends in a vowel, so it takes 야. Casual, for close friends.
At work or with new people
라일리 씨
라일리 ssi. Polite, roughly "Mr" or "Ms", but used with the first name.
Customer, client, online
라일리 님
라일리 nim. What you'll see on a delivery box or a booking.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables (after a one-syllable family name), so at 3 syllables 라일리 reads as a foreign name at a glance. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 라일리 or 라일리 씨.

Spelled letter-by-letter you'd get 릴리, but Korean media write 라일리, because the name is written the way it sounds, not the way it's spelled.

  • An l between vowels is written twice (final ㄹ + ㄹ), so Koreans say a clear l instead of a tapped r.

Questions people ask

How do you write Riley in Korean?

Riley is written 라일리 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: railli). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has three syllables: 라 + 일 + 리.

How do you pronounce Riley in Korean?

Say it ra-il-li, with even weight on every syllable. The opening ㄹ is a single light tap, halfway between an English r and l. Never roll it.
