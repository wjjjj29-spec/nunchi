Donald in Korean 도널드

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Donald as 도널드: three syllables, said do-neol-deu. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Pronunciation tip

드 carries the filler vowel ㅡ (eu). Keep it short and light, almost just the consonant.

  • ㄹ at the bottom of 널 is an l: the tongue touches just behind the teeth and stays there.
  • ㅓ (eo) in 널 is the open "uh" of "sun", not an "o".
  • ㄷ at the start is soft and unvoiced, halfway between t and d.
  • ㅗ is a pure "o" with rounded lips. Don't add the w-glide English puts in "go".

How Koreans would call you

Friends calling you over
도널드야!
도널드 ends in a vowel, so it takes 야. Casual, for close friends.
At work or with new people
도널드 씨
도널드 ssi. Polite, roughly "Mr" or "Ms", but used with the first name.
Customer, client, online
도널드 님
도널드 nim. What you'll see on a delivery box or a booking.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables (after a one-syllable family name), so at 3 syllables 도널드 reads as a foreign name at a glance. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 도널드 or 도널드 씨.

Why it's spelled 도널드

How Koreans write it

Spelled letter-by-letter you'd get 도낼드, but Korean media write 도널드, because the name is written the way it sounds, not the way it's spelled.

  • The unstressed "uh" vowel is written ㅓ (eo), whatever letter English uses for it.
  • Korean adds the vowel ㅡ to consonants that have no vowel of their own (드), which is why Donald has 3 syllables in Korean.

Questions people ask

How do you write Donald in Korean?

Donald is written 도널드 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: doneoldeu). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has three syllables: 도 + 널 + 드.

How do you pronounce Donald in Korean?

Say it do-neol-deu, with even weight on every syllable. 드 carries the filler vowel ㅡ (eu). Keep it short and light, almost just the consonant.
