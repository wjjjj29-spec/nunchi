Zara in Korean 자라

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Zara as 자라: two syllables, said ja-ra. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Pronunciation tip

ㄹ between two vowels (자라) is one quick tap of the tongue, like the tt in American "butter": closer to a soft d than an English r.

  • ㅈ at the start is soft and unvoiced, halfway between ch and j.
  • Two even beats, ja-ra: Korean doesn't stress one syllable over the other the way English does.

How Koreans would call you

Friends calling you over
자라야!
자라 ends in a vowel, so it takes 야. Casual, for close friends.
At work or with new people
자라 씨
자라 ssi. Polite, roughly "Mr" or "Ms", but used with the first name.
Customer, client, online
자라 님
자라 nim. What you'll see on a delivery box or a booking.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables, so 자라 already sounds natural to Korean ears. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 자라 or 자라 씨.

Good to know

자라 is the Korean word for a soft-shelled turtle.

Why it's spelled 자라

Here the letters and the sound line up: applying the loanword rules letter by letter already gives 자라, the same spelling Korean media use.

  • Korean has no z, so z becomes ㅈ (j).

Questions people ask

How do you write Zara in Korean?

Zara is written 자라 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: jara). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has two syllables: 자 + 라.

How do you pronounce Zara in Korean?

Say it ja-ra, with even weight on every syllable. ㄹ between two vowels (자라) is one quick tap of the tongue, like the tt in American "butter": closer to a soft d than an English r.
