아치 nim. What you'll see on a delivery box or a booking.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables, so 아치 already sounds natural to Korean ears. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 아치 or 아치 씨.

Why it's spelled 아치

Here the letters and the sound line up: applying the loanword rules letter by letter already gives 아치, the same spelling Korean media use.

  • An r after a vowel (before a consonant or at the end) isn't written. Korean spelling follows British-style pronunciation, where that r is silent.

Questions people ask

How do you write Archie in Korean?

Archie is written 아치 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: achi). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has two syllables: 아 + 치.

How do you pronounce Archie in Korean?

Say it a-chi, with even weight on every syllable. Two even beats, a-chi: Korean doesn't stress one syllable over the other the way English does.
