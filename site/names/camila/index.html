Camila in Korean 카밀라

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Camila as 카밀라: three syllables, said ka-mil-la. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Pronunciation tip

The double ㄹ in 밀라 is a clear English l, as in "hello", not an r.

  • ㅋ at the start is aspirated: a clear puff of air, like the k in "kite".
  • Give all 3 syllables the same weight. Korean has no strong stress, so it's ka-mil-la, evenly.

How Koreans would call you

Friends calling you over
카밀라야!
카밀라 ends in a vowel, so it takes 야. Casual, for close friends.
At work or with new people
카밀라 씨
카밀라 ssi. Polite, roughly "Mr" or "Ms", but used with the first name.
Customer, client, online
카밀라 님
카밀라 nim. What you'll see on a delivery box or a booking.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables (after a one-syllable family name), so at 3 syllables 카밀라 reads as a foreign name at a glance. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 카밀라 or 카밀라 씨.

Why it's spelled 카밀라

Here the letters and the sound line up: applying the loanword rules letter by letter already gives 카밀라, the same spelling Korean media use.

  • Korean writes Spanish names by their Spanish sound: vowels are read as written.

Questions people ask

How do you write Camila in Korean?

Camila is written 카밀라 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: kamilla). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name and follows the Spanish pronunciation. In Hangul it has three syllables: 카 + 밀 + 라.

How do you pronounce Camila in Korean?

Say it ka-mil-la, with even weight on every syllable. The double ㄹ in 밀라 is a clear English l, as in "hello", not an r.
