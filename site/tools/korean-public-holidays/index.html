Nunchi tip

Seollal and Chuseok are the two holidays that change your week. Trains sell out weeks ahead, the highways are a car park, and the small restaurant you love will be shut. Book early or stay put and enjoy a quiet, empty Seoul.

What closes, what does not

Government offices, immigration, banks, post offices and schools close on every public holiday. Hospitals keep emergency rooms open and many pharmacies open on rotation (look for 휴일지킴이약국). Shops, cafés, cinemas, convenience stores and palaces are open on ordinary holidays and usually busier than usual.

Seollal (Lunar New Year) and Chuseok (harvest festival) are different. They are family holidays and for two or three days many small restaurants, markets and family businesses close. Large chains, department stores on some days, and tourist districts stay open. Public transport runs, but intercity trains and buses sell out well ahead.

Substitute holidays

When a holiday lands on a weekend, Korea usually gives you the next weekday off instead. This applies to Independence Movement Day, Children's Day, Buddha's Birthday, Liberation Day, National Foundation Day, Hangul Day and Christmas, and to Seollal and Chuseok when one of their three days is a Sunday. New Year's Day and Memorial Day get no substitute; if they fall on a Saturday, that is that.

Questions people ask

Is Christmas a public holiday in Korea?

Yes, 25 December is a national holiday. Christmas Eve is a normal working day, and so is 26 December.

Do I get paid for public holidays?

If you are an employee at a company with five or more staff, public holidays are paid days off by law. Working on one means holiday pay at 150 percent or a day off in lieu. Working-holiday and part-time workers should check their contract.

Are election days holidays?

Yes. National election days are public holidays so people can vote. The 2026 local elections on 3 June are a holiday. There is no national election scheduled in 2027.

What are the public holidays in Korea in May 2026?

Children's Day on Tuesday 5 May, Buddha's Birthday on Sunday 24 May, and a substitute holiday on Monday 25 May. See May 2026.

When is Chuseok in 2026?

Friday 25 September, with the holiday running Thursday 24 to Saturday 26 September. No substitute day, because the Saturday does not trigger one. See September 2026.

What are the public holidays in Korea in October 2026?

National Foundation Day on Saturday 3 October, its substitute on Monday 5 October, and Hangul Day on Friday 9 October: two long weekends back to back. See October 2026.

Where do these dates come from?

The Ministry of the Interior and Safety publishes the official list each year. Lunar holidays follow the Korean lunar calendar and we check them against the official announcement.
