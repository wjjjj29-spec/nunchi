Evan in Korean 에번

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Evan as 에번: two syllables, said e-beon. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Pronunciation tip

ㅓ (eo) in 번 is the open "uh" of "sun", not an "o".

  • Two even beats, e-beon: Korean doesn't stress one syllable over the other the way English does.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables, so 에번 already sounds natural to Korean ears. In casual sentences Koreans often add 이 after a name that ends in a consonant: 에번이 왔어 ("Evan's here").

Why it's spelled 에번

How Koreans write it
에번
the settled media spelling

Spelled letter-by-letter you'd get 에밴, but Korean media write 에번, because the name is written the way it sounds, not the way it's spelled.

  • There is no v in Korean either: v becomes ㅂ (b).
  • The unstressed "uh" vowel is written ㅓ (eo), whatever letter English uses for it.

Questions people ask

How do you write Evan in Korean?

Evan is written 에번 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: ebeon). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has two syllables: 에 + 번.

How do you pronounce Evan in Korean?

Say it e-beon, with even weight on every syllable. ㅓ (eo) in 번 is the open "uh" of "sun", not an "o".
