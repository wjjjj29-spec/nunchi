Pronunciation tip

The double ㄹ in 칼릿 is a clear English l, as in "hello", not an r.

  • 스 carries the filler vowel ㅡ (eu). Keep it short and light, almost just the consonant.
  • The final ㅅ in 릿 is unreleased: stop the sound (t) without a puff of air. It is a t sound here, not s.
  • Give all 3 syllables the same weight. Korean has no strong stress, so it's seu-kal-lit, evenly.

Why it's spelled 스칼릿

Spelled letter-by-letter you'd get 스칼렛, but Korean media write 스칼릿, because the name is written the way it sounds, not the way it's spelled.

  • An r after a vowel (before a consonant or at the end) isn't written. Korean spelling follows British-style pronunciation, where that r is silent.
  • An l between vowels is written twice (final ㄹ + ㄹ), so Koreans say a clear l instead of a tapped r.
  • A final t is written with ㅅ under the syllable. It's still said as a held t.
  • Double letters count once. Hangul spells the sound, not the letters.
  • Korean adds the vowel ㅡ to consonants that have no vowel of their own (스), which is why Scarlett has 3 syllables in Korean.
