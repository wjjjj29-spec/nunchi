Dimas in Korean 디마스

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Dimas as 디마스: three syllables, said di-ma-seu. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Pronunciation tip

스 carries the filler vowel ㅡ (eu). Keep it short and light, almost just the consonant.

  • ㄷ at the start is soft and unvoiced, halfway between t and d.
  • Give all 3 syllables the same weight. Korean has no strong stress, so it's di-ma-seu, evenly.

디마스 nim. What you'll see on a delivery box or a booking.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables (after a one-syllable family name), so at 3 syllables 디마스 reads as a foreign name at a glance. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 디마스 or 디마스 씨.

Spelled letter-by-letter you'd get 디매스, but Korean media write 디마스, because the name is written the way it sounds, not the way it's spelled.

  • Korean adds the vowel ㅡ to consonants that have no vowel of their own (스), which is why Dimas has 3 syllables in Korean.

Questions people ask

How do you write Dimas in Korean?

Dimas is written 디마스 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: dimaseu). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has three syllables: 디 + 마 + 스.

How do you pronounce Dimas in Korean?

Say it di-ma-seu, with even weight on every syllable. 스 carries the filler vowel ㅡ (eu). Keep it short and light, almost just the consonant.

What does Dimas mean in Korean?

On its own, nothing: 디마스 spells the sound of Dimas, so the syllables are not Korean words and Koreans read it simply as a foreign name.
