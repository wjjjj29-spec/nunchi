Pablo in Korean 파블로

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Pablo as 파블로: three syllables, said pa-beul-lo. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Pronunciation tip

The double ㄹ in 블로 is a clear English l, as in "hello", not an r.

  • 블 carries the filler vowel ㅡ (eu). Keep it short and light, almost just the consonant.
  • ㅍ at the start is aspirated: a clear puff of air, like the p in "pie".
  • ㅗ is a pure "o" with rounded lips. Don't add the w-glide English puts in "go".
  • Give all 3 syllables the same weight. Korean has no strong stress, so it's pa-beul-lo, evenly.

How Koreans would call you

Friends calling you over
파블로야!
파블로 ends in a vowel, so it takes 야. Casual, for close friends.
At work or with new people
파블로 씨
파블로 ssi. Polite, roughly "Mr" or "Ms", but used with the first name.
Customer, client, online
파블로 님
파블로 nim. What you'll see on a delivery box or a booking.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables (after a one-syllable family name), so at 3 syllables 파블로 reads as a foreign name at a glance. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 파블로 or 파블로 씨.

Spelled letter-by-letter you'd get 패브로, but Korean media write 파블로, because the name is written the way it sounds in Spanish, not the way it's spelled.

  • Korean writes Spanish names by their Spanish sound: vowels are read as written.

Questions people ask

How do you write Pablo in Korean?

Pablo is written 파블로 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: pabeullo). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name and follows the Spanish pronunciation. In Hangul it has three syllables: 파 + 블 + 로.

How do you pronounce Pablo in Korean?

Say it pa-beul-lo, with even weight on every syllable. The double ㄹ in 블로 is a clear English l, as in "hello", not an r.
