Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Ashwin as 아슈윈: three syllables, said a-syu-win. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Pronunciation tip

ㅟ in 윈 is one gliding vowel: wee, in a single beat.

  • ㅇ at the top of 윈 is silent, so 윈 is just the vowel: win.
  • Give all 3 syllables the same weight. Korean has no strong stress, so it's a-syu-win, evenly.

Questions people ask

How do you write Ashwin in Korean?

Ashwin is written 아슈윈 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: asyuwin). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has three syllables: 아 + 슈 + 윈.

How do you pronounce Ashwin in Korean?

Say it a-syu-win, with even weight on every syllable. ㅟ in 윈 is one gliding vowel: wee, in a single beat.

What does Ashwin mean in Korean?

On its own, nothing: 아슈윈 spells the sound of Ashwin, so the syllables are not Korean words and Koreans read it simply as a foreign name.
