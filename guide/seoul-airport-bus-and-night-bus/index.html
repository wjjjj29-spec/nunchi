Airport limousine buses and Seoul's late-night Owl buses

The bus that drops you at your hotel door, where to catch it at Incheon, and how to get home at 2am once the subway has stopped.

3 min readChecked 28 October 2026
At a glance1 / 6
The short version
  • Limousine buses: from Incheon to most of Seoul for ₩17,000–18,000, many stopping outside big hotels. Buy a ticket at the counter or kiosk before you board.
  • Where: Terminal 1 arrivals level, outside exits 3–7. Terminal 2, B1 of the Transportation Center, stops 11–35.
  • Late flights: N6000, N6002, N6701 and N6703 run from both terminals after midnight, ₩17,000–18,000.
  • In the city after midnight: Seoul's 14 Owl bus routes (N13 to N75) run from about 11pm to 4am for ₩2,500 by card. The Climate Card covers them.

Incheon limousine buses

A limousine bus (공항 리무진, gonghang rimujin) is a coach with a big luggage hold and reclining seats. Two companies run most Seoul routes, and they price slightly differently:

  • Airport Limousine runs the 6000-series routes such as 6001 (Dongdaemun and Myeongdong), 6002 (Cheongnyangni and Hongdae), 6005 (Seoul City Hall), 6007 (Yeouido) and 6015 (Myeongdong, Euljiro, Mapo). Route 6001 is ₩17,000.
  • K Limousine runs 6701 (City Hall and Gwanghwamun), 6702 (Namsan and Dongdaemun), 6703 (Gangnam and COEX) and 6705 (Jamsil and Dong Seoul), all ₩18,000. Route 6701 stops outside hotels such as The Plaza and the Westin Josun, and leaves Terminal 1 every 25–35 minutes from 5:35am to 10:25pm.

Allow at least an hour into central Seoul, more in rush hour. Before you queue, tell the ticket desk your hotel or nearest station. Staff will name the right route. If you'd rather point at a phrase: 이 버스 명동 가요? (i beoseu myeongdong gayo?, "does this bus go to Myeongdong?").

Where to catch it

  • Terminal 1: buses to Seoul leave from the arrivals level (1F), outside exits 3 to 7. Ticket desks are inside arrivals next to exits 4 and 9, and outside near exits 4, 6, 7, 8, 11 and 13.
  • Terminal 2: everything is on B1 of the Transportation Center. Seoul routes use stops 11 to 35 in the middle section, with a staffed ticket counter in the waiting room and kiosks on both sides.

How to pay

Leaving the airport, buy a ticket first. K Limousine only accepts tickets issued at the counter at Incheon, with no cash or transit card on board, and a QR ticket bought online must be swapped for a paper one at the desk. Airport Limousine says its buses take a transit card or cash on board, but at the airport a ticket from the desk is the safe bet.

Going to the airport is easier: on K Limousine you tap T-money or a card at the reader as you board, and seats are first come, first served. T-money gets you a receipt. A card payment doesn't. Not sure which transit card to buy? Try the T-money vs Climate Card tool.

Nunchi tip

Flying out early? Check the timetable from your hotel stop the night before. Routes like 6001 start in the city from about 4:30am, and the first coaches fill up with people heading for morning flights.

Gimpo Airport

Gimpo is inside Seoul and on the subway, so most travellers skip the bus: subway Lines 5 and 9 and the AREX airport railway all stop there. A few Incheon limousine routes, such as 6003 and 6014, also call at Gimpo, which helps if you're changing between the two airports with heavy bags.

Owl buses: Seoul after the subway

The subway shuts around midnight. After that, Seoul runs 14 late-night city bus routes, the Owl buses (올빼미버스, olppaemi beoseu). Look for the big N before the number. They cost ₩2,500 by card, run on roughly 11pm–4am timetables, and the Climate Card covers them.

The times are the first and last buses the city lists for each route, so a bus reaches stops further along later. Check live arrivals in Naver Map or Kakao Map, or on the screens at the stop. Changing between two Owl routes within an hour is free.

Missed the last one, or it's a long walk from the stop? A taxi is the backup. Between 11pm and 2am the meter runs 40% higher.

Questions people ask

How much is the limousine bus from Incheon Airport to Seoul?

Most routes cost ₩17,000–18,000 for adults in 2026. Airport Limousine's 6001 to Myeongdong is ₩17,000. K Limousine's 6701–6705 routes are ₩18,000.

Can I pay for the Incheon airport bus with T-money?

From the airport, buy a ticket at the counter or kiosk first. K Limousine doesn't take cash or transit cards at Incheon. On the way to the airport you can tap T-money or a card when you board.

Where do I catch the airport bus at Incheon Terminal 2?

On B1 of the Transportation Center. Seoul routes leave from stops 11 to 35, and tickets are sold at the counter in the waiting room and at kiosks.

Is there a bus from Incheon Airport after midnight?

Yes. N6000 (Gangnam Express Bus Terminal, ₩17,000), N6002 (Hongdae and Cheongnyangni), N6701 (Seoul Station and DDP) and N6703 (COEX and Jamsil), ₩18,000, run from both terminals.

How do night buses work in Seoul?

Seoul's Owl buses are 14 N routes running about 11pm to 4am for ₩2,500 by card. N75 links Hongdae, Gwanghwamun, Seoul Station and Gangnam. The Climate Card covers them.
