Riya in Korean 리야

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Riya as 리야: two syllables, said ri-ya. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Riya in Hangul
리야
Revised Romanization: riya · said ri-ya · two syllables

Riya letter by letter

Each Hangul block is one syllable, built from a consonant, a vowel and sometimes a final consonant underneath.

리
ㄹ r/l + ㅣ i
ri
야
ㅇ silent + ㅑ ya
ya
Pronunciation tip

The opening ㄹ is a single light tap, halfway between an English r and l. Never roll it.

  • ㅇ at the top of 야 is silent, so 야 is just the vowel: ya.
  • Two even beats, ri-ya: Korean doesn't stress one syllable over the other the way English does.

How Koreans would call you

Friends calling you over

리야 ends in a vowel, so it takes 야. Casual, for close friends.
At work or with new people
리야 씨
리야 ssi. Polite, roughly "Mr" or "Ms", but used with the first name.
Customer, client, online
리야 님
리야 nim. What you'll see on a delivery box or a booking.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables, so 리야 already sounds natural to Korean ears. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 리야 or 리야 씨.

Why it's spelled 리야

Here the letters and the sound line up: applying the loanword rules letter by letter already gives 리야, the same spelling Korean media use.

Questions people ask

How do you write Riya in Korean?

Riya is written 리야 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: riya). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has two syllables: 리 + 야.

How do you pronounce Riya in Korean?

Say it ri-ya, with even weight on every syllable. The opening ㄹ is a single light tap, halfway between an English r and l. Never roll it.

What does Riya mean in Korean?

On its own, nothing: 리야 spells the sound of Riya, so the syllables are not Korean words and Koreans read it simply as a foreign name.
