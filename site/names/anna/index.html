Anna in Korean 애나

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Anna as 애나: two syllables, said ae-na. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Anna in Hangul
애나
Revised Romanization: aena · said ae-na · two syllables

Anna letter by letter

Each Hangul block is one syllable, built from a consonant, a vowel and sometimes a final consonant underneath.

Pronunciation tip

ㅐ (ae) is said like the e in "bed" by most Koreans today, a little more closed than the a in "cat".

  • Two even beats, ae-na: Korean doesn't stress one syllable over the other the way English does.

How Koreans would call you

Friends calling you over
애나야!
애나 ends in a vowel, so it takes 야. Casual, for close friends.
At work or with new people
애나 씨
애나 ssi. Polite, roughly "Mr" or "Ms", but used with the first name.
Customer, client, online
애나 님
애나 nim. What you'll see on a delivery box or a booking.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables, so 애나 already sounds natural to Korean ears. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 애나 or 애나 씨.

Why it's spelled 애나

Here the letters and the sound line up: applying the loanword rules letter by letter already gives 애나, the same spelling Korean media use.

  • Double letters count once. Hangul spells the sound, not the letters.

Questions people ask

How do you write Anna in Korean?

Anna is written 애나 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: aena). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has two syllables: 애 + 나.

How do you pronounce Anna in Korean?

Say it ae-na, with even weight on every syllable. ㅐ (ae) is said like the e in "bed" by most Koreans today, a little more closed than the a in "cat".
