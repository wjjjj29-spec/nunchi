히나 nim. What you'll see on a delivery box or a booking.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables, so 히나 already sounds natural to Korean ears. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 히나 or 히나 씨.

Why it's spelled 히나

Here the letters and the sound line up: applying the loanword rules letter by letter already gives 히나, the same spelling Korean media use.

  • Korean writes Japanese names with its own Japanese table: each Japanese syllable maps to one Hangul syllable and long vowels are not marked.

Questions people ask

How do you write Hina in Korean?

Hina is written 히나 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: hina). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name and follows the Japanese pronunciation. In Hangul it has two syllables: 히 + 나.

How do you pronounce Hina in Korean?

Say it hi-na, with even weight on every syllable. Two even beats, hi-na: Korean doesn't stress one syllable over the other the way English does.
