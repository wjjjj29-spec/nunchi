Cheryl in Korean 셰릴

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Cheryl as 셰릴: two syllables, said sye-ril. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Pronunciation tip

ㄹ between two vowels (셰릴) is one quick tap of the tongue, like the tt in American "butter": closer to a soft d than an English r.

  • ㄹ at the bottom of 릴 is an l: the tongue touches just behind the teeth and stays there.
  • Two even beats, sye-ril: Korean doesn't stress one syllable over the other the way English does.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables, so 셰릴 already sounds natural to Korean ears. In casual sentences Koreans often add 이 after a name that ends in a consonant: 셰릴이 왔어 ("Cheryl's here").

Questions people ask

How do you write Cheryl in Korean?

Cheryl is written 셰릴 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: syeril). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has two syllables: 셰 + 릴.

How do you pronounce Cheryl in Korean?

Say it sye-ril, with even weight on every syllable. ㄹ between two vowels (셰릴) is one quick tap of the tongue, like the tt in American "butter": closer to a soft d than an English r.
