Virginia in Korean 버지니아

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Virginia as 버지니아: four syllables, said beo-ji-ni-a. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Pronunciation tip

ㅓ (eo) in 버 is the open "uh" of "sun", not an "o".

  • ㅂ at the start is soft and unvoiced, halfway between p and b.
  • ㅇ at the top of 아 is silent, so 아 is just the vowel: a.
  • Give all 4 syllables the same weight. Korean has no strong stress, so it's beo-ji-ni-a, evenly.

How Koreans would call you

Friends calling you over
버지니아야!
버지니아 ends in a vowel, so it takes 야. Casual, for close friends.
At work or with new people
버지니아 씨
버지니아 ssi. Polite, roughly "Mr" or "Ms", but used with the first name.
Customer, client, online
버지니아 님
버지니아 nim. What you'll see on a delivery box or a booking.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables (after a one-syllable family name), so at 4 syllables 버지니아 reads as a foreign name at a glance. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 버지니아 or 버지니아 씨.

Why it's spelled 버지니아

Here the letters and the sound line up: applying the loanword rules letter by letter already gives 버지니아, the same spelling Korean media use.

  • There is no v in Korean either: v becomes ㅂ (b).
  • An r after a vowel (before a consonant or at the end) isn't written. Korean spelling follows British-style pronunciation, where that r is silent.
  • The unstressed "uh" vowel is written ㅓ (eo), whatever letter English uses for it.

Questions people ask

How do you write Virginia in Korean?

Virginia is written 버지니아 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: beojinia). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has four syllables: 버 + 지 + 니 + 아.

How do you pronounce Virginia in Korean?

Say it beo-ji-ni-a, with even weight on every syllable. ㅓ (eo) in 버 is the open "uh" of "sun", not an "o".
