Raj in Korean 라지

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Raj as 라지: two syllables, said ra-ji. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Raj letter by letter

Pronunciation tip

The opening ㄹ is a single light tap, halfway between an English r and l. Never roll it.

  • Two even beats, ra-ji: Korean doesn't stress one syllable over the other the way English does.

How Koreans would call you

At work or with new people

라지 ssi. Polite, roughly "Mr" or "Ms", but used with the first name.
Customer, client, online
라지 님
라지 nim. What you'll see on a delivery box or a booking.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables, so 라지 already sounds natural to Korean ears. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 라지 or 라지 씨.

How Koreans write it
라지
the settled media spelling

Spelled letter-by-letter you'd get 래지, but Korean media write 라지, because the name is written the way it sounds in Hindi, not the way it's spelled.

  • Korean follows the Hindi sound: vowels are read as written and the r is pronounced (ㄹ/르) rather than dropped as in English.

Questions people ask

How do you write Raj in Korean?

Raj is written 라지 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: raji). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name and follows the Hindi pronunciation. In Hangul it has two syllables: 라 + 지.

How do you pronounce Raj in Korean?

Say it ra-ji, with even weight on every syllable. The opening ㄹ is a single light tap, halfway between an English r and l. Never roll it.
