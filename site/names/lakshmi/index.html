Lakshmi in Korean 락슈미

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Lakshmi as 락슈미: three syllables, said rak-syu-mi. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Pronunciation tip

The opening ㄹ is a single light tap, halfway between an English r and l. Never roll it.

  • The final ㄱ in 락 is unreleased: stop the sound (k) without a puff of air.
  • Give all 3 syllables the same weight. Korean has no strong stress, so it's rak-syu-mi, evenly.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables (after a one-syllable family name), so at 3 syllables 락슈미 reads as a foreign name at a glance. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 락슈미 or 락슈미 씨.

Questions people ask

How do you write Lakshmi in Korean?

Lakshmi is written 락슈미 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: raksyumi). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has three syllables: 락 + 슈 + 미.

How do you pronounce Lakshmi in Korean?

Say it rak-syu-mi, with even weight on every syllable. The opening ㄹ is a single light tap, halfway between an English r and l. Never roll it.

What does Lakshmi mean in Korean?

On its own, nothing: 락슈미 spells the sound of Lakshmi, so the syllables are not Korean words and Koreans read it simply as a foreign name.
