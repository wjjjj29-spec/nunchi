Where to stay in Seoul: picking a neighbourhood for your first trip

Seven neighbourhoods, who each one suits, how you get there from Incheon and what the evenings are like, in 2026.

4 min readChecked 24 October 2026
At a glance1 / 6
The short version
  • First trip: Myeongdong or Seoul Station. Central, easy from the airport, palaces and markets nearby.
  • Nightlife on a budget: Hongdae. AREX runs straight there from Incheon for ₩4,650.
  • Culture and quiet: Insadong and Jongno, with hanok stays. Part of Bukchon is closed to tourists 5pm–10am.
  • Second trip: Seongsu for cafés and pop-ups, Gangnam for big hotels and COEX, Itaewon for bars and international food.

The short answer

For a first trip, stay in Myeongdong or near Seoul Station: central, easy from the airport, and close to the palaces, markets and Namsan. Pick Hongdae for nightlife and the cheapest airport train, Insadong or Jongno for palaces and quiet evenings, and Seongsu if you came for cafés and shopping. Wherever you book, the thing that matters most is being a few minutes' walk from a subway station, ideally on Line 2 or the airport railroad (AREX).

Myeongdong: the easy default

Shops, skincare stores and money changers by day, street-food stalls in the evening, and Namdaemun Market, Deoksugung and the Namsan cable car within walking distance. Myeongdong Station is on Line 4, two stops from Seoul Station, and Line 2 is a short walk away at Euljiro 1-ga. Limousine bus 6001 stops at Myeongdong Station for ₩17,000. The downside: it's crowded and very touristy, and hotels fill up. Big Myeongdong hotels ran at about 85–90% occupancy in the first half of 2026.

Hongdae: nightlife and the cheapest airport ride

Around Hongik University: clubs, bars, buskers, vintage shops and food until late, with plenty of hostels and guesthouses. Hongik Univ. Station is on Line 2, AREX and the Gyeongui-Jungang Line, so the AREX all-stop train takes you straight from the airport for ₩4,650 from Terminal 1 (₩5,250 from Terminal 2). Weekend nights are loud. Light sleepers should pick a street a few minutes away from the main strip, or ask for a high floor: 높은 층으로 주세요 (nopeun cheung-euro juseyo, "a high floor, please").

Seongsu: cafés and pop-ups

A former factory district by Seoul Forest, now full of cafés, concept stores, K-beauty shops and pop-up stores (about 70–100 new pop-ups opened each month in early 2026). Foreign visitors to its main shopping streets rose about 74% in January–April 2026 compared with a year earlier. Stations: Seongsu (Line 2) and Seoul Forest (Suin-Bundang Line). There's no direct airport train: take AREX to Hongik Univ. and ride Line 2 across town, and allow well over an hour. Better as a base for a second trip than a first.

Insadong and Jongno: palaces and quiet nights

Gyeongbokgung, Changdeokgung, Bukchon and the tea houses of Insadong are all on foot, and there are hanok guesthouses (한옥, hanok, a traditional Korean house) if you want to sleep in one. Stations: Anguk (Line 3), Jonggak (Line 1) and Jongno 3-ga (Lines 1, 3 and 5). From the airport, take AREX to Seoul Station and Line 1 two stops to Jonggak. Evenings are quiet and some streets empty out early.

Part of Bukchon (Bukchon-ro 11-gil, the "red zone") has been closed to tourists from 5pm to 10am since March 2025, with a ₩100,000 fine. Guests staying in hanok there are exempt.

Gangnam: polished, but far from the palaces

Big hotels, COEX and the Starfield Library, department stores, clinics and upmarket bars. Gangnam Station is on Line 2 and the Shinbundang Line. AREX doesn't reach it, so take a limousine bus to Gangnam or COEX, or AREX to Gimpo Airport and the Line 9 express to Sinnonhyeon. The palaces and old city are north of the river, a long subway ride away.

Itaewon: food and bars

Seoul's most international area, with restaurants from everywhere and rooftop bars. It's hilly, and busy late at weekends. Itaewon Station is on Line 6: take AREX to Gongdeok and change to Line 6, four stops east.

Seoul Station: for early flights and day trips

Not the prettiest neighbourhood, but the most practical. AREX Express runs non-stop to Terminal 1 in 43 minutes for ₩13,000 (51 minutes to Terminal 2), KTX trains leave for Busan and Gyeongju, and Lines 1 and 4 take you into the centre. Express riders can check in and drop bags at the City Airport Terminal in the station. If you're flying out early or doing day trips by train, spend your last night here.

Prices and booking

Seoul is busy in 2026, and central hotels fill early. Two Myeongdong hotels tracked by the Financial News averaged about ₩261,000 and ₩283,000 a night from January to May 2026, up about 6–12% on 2025. Hongdae has the widest choice of hostels and guesthouses, and Gangnam the most luxury hotels. Work out your total in our trip budget calculator, and read our Incheon Airport to Seoul guide before you book, because the airport ride is where a neighbourhood choice really shows.

Nunchi tip

Before booking, find the hotel in Naver Map and check which station exit it's nearest. "Near Myeongdong" can mean a 15-minute walk uphill with your suitcase. Within 5 minutes of an exit with a lift is the sweet spot.

Questions people ask

What is the best area to stay in Seoul for first-time visitors?

Myeongdong is the easy default: central, on Line 4, near Namdaemun Market and the palaces, with limousine bus 6001 from the airport. Seoul Station is just as practical and best for early flights.

Is Hongdae or Myeongdong better?

Hongdae for nightlife, younger crowds and cheaper rooms, with a direct AREX train from the airport. Myeongdong for shopping and being central, but it's busier and more touristy.

Is Gangnam a good place to stay in Seoul?

It's good for COEX, business and big hotels, but it's south of the river, a long subway ride from the palaces. AREX doesn't go there, so use a limousine bus or AREX plus the Line 9 express from Gimpo Airport.

Which Seoul area is easiest from Incheon Airport?

Seoul Station (AREX Express, 43 minutes, ₩13,000) and Hongdae (AREX all-stop, no change, ₩4,650 from Terminal 1). Myeongdong has a direct limousine bus.

Can I stay in a hanok in Bukchon?

Yes. Hanok guesthouses in Bukchon and around Insadong take guests, and guests are exempt from Bukchon's 5pm–10am tourist restriction in the red zone.
