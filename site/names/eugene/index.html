Eugene in Korean 유진

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Eugene as 유진: two syllables, said yu-jin. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Eugene in Hangul

Revised Romanization: yujin · said yu-jin · two syllables

Eugene letter by letter

Each Hangul block is one syllable, built from a consonant, a vowel and sometimes a final consonant underneath.

Pronunciation tip

Two even beats, yu-jin: Korean doesn't stress one syllable over the other the way English does.

How Koreans would call you

Friends calling you over
유진아!
유진 ends in a consonant, so it takes 아, said 유지나. Casual, for close friends.
At work or with new people
유진 씨
유진 ssi. Polite, roughly "Mr" or "Ms", but used with the first name.
Customer, client, online
유진 님
유진 nim. What you'll see on a delivery box or a booking.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables, so 유진 already sounds natural to Korean ears. In casual sentences Koreans often add 이 after a name that ends in a consonant: 유진이 왔어 ("Eugene's here").

Good to know

유진 (Yu-jin) is also a popular Korean given name, so Koreans may assume you have a Korean name.

Why it's spelled 유진

Here the letters and the sound line up: applying the loanword rules letter by letter already gives 유진, the same spelling Korean media use.

  • The final e is silent. It only makes the vowel before it long.

Questions people ask

How do you write Eugene in Korean?

Eugene is written 유진 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: yujin). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has two syllables: 유 + 진.

How do you pronounce Eugene in Korean?

Say it yu-jin, with even weight on every syllable. Two even beats, yu-jin: Korean doesn't stress one syllable over the other the way English does.
