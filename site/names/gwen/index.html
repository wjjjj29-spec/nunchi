Gwen in Korean 그웬

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Gwen as 그웬: two syllables, said geu-wen. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Gwen letter by letter

Pronunciation tip

그 carries the filler vowel ㅡ (eu). Keep it short and light, almost just the consonant.

  • ㅞ in 웬 is one gliding vowel: we, in a single beat.
  • ㄱ at the start is soft and unvoiced, halfway between k and g.
  • ㅇ at the top of 웬 is silent, so 웬 is just the vowel: wen.
  • Two even beats, geu-wen: Korean doesn't stress one syllable over the other the way English does.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables, so 그웬 already sounds natural to Korean ears. In casual sentences Koreans often add 이 after a name that ends in a consonant: 그웬이 왔어 ("Gwen's here").

Why it's spelled 그웬

Here the letters and the sound line up: applying the loanword rules letter by letter already gives 그웬, the same spelling Korean media use.

  • It is a Welsh name, and Welsh spelling has its own rules. Korean writes the Welsh sound.

Questions people ask

How do you write Gwen in Korean?

Gwen is written 그웬 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: geuwen). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name and follows the Welsh pronunciation. In Hangul it has two syllables: 그 + 웬.

How do you pronounce Gwen in Korean?

Say it geu-wen, with even weight on every syllable. 그 carries the filler vowel ㅡ (eu). Keep it short and light, almost just the consonant.
