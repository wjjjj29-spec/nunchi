Gemma in Korean 젬마

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Gemma as 젬마: two syllables, said jem-ma. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Gemma letter by letter

Each Hangul block is one syllable, built from a consonant, a vowel and sometimes a final consonant underneath.

Pronunciation tip

ㅈ at the start is soft and unvoiced, halfway between ch and j.

  • Two even beats, jem-ma: Korean doesn't stress one syllable over the other the way English does.

How Koreans would call you

Friends calling you over
젬마야!
젬마 ends in a vowel, so it takes 야. Casual, for close friends.
At work or with new people
젬마 씨
젬마 ssi. Polite, roughly "Mr" or "Ms", but used with the first name.
Customer, client, online
젬마 님
젬마 nim. What you'll see on a delivery box or a booking.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables, so 젬마 already sounds natural to Korean ears. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 젬마 or 젬마 씨.

Spelled letter-by-letter you'd get 제마, but Korean media write 젬마, because the name is written the way it sounds, not the way it's spelled.

  • Double letters count once. Hangul spells the sound, not the letters.

Questions people ask

How do you write Gemma in Korean?

Gemma is written 젬마 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: jemma). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has two syllables: 젬 + 마.

How do you pronounce Gemma in Korean?

Say it jem-ma, with even weight on every syllable. ㅈ at the start is soft and unvoiced, halfway between ch and j.
