Randy in Korean 랜디

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Randy as 랜디: two syllables, said raen-di. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Randy letter by letter

Each Hangul block is one syllable, built from a consonant, a vowel and sometimes a final consonant underneath.

랜
ㄹ r/l + ㅐ ae + ㄴ n (final)
raen
디
ㄷ d + ㅣ i
di
Pronunciation tip

The opening ㄹ is a single light tap, halfway between an English r and l. Never roll it.

  • ㅐ (ae) is said like the e in "bed" by most Koreans today, a little more closed than the a in "cat".
  • Two even beats, raen-di: Korean doesn't stress one syllable over the other the way English does.

How Koreans would call you

Friends calling you over
랜디야!
랜디 ends in a vowel, so it takes 야. Casual, for close friends.
At work or with new people
랜디 씨
랜디 ssi. Polite, roughly "Mr" or "Ms", but used with the first name.
Customer, client, online
랜디 님
랜디 nim. What you'll see on a delivery box or a booking.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables, so 랜디 already sounds natural to Korean ears. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 랜디 or 랜디 씨.

Why it's spelled 랜디

Here the letters and the sound line up: applying the loanword rules letter by letter already gives 랜디, the same spelling Korean media use.

Questions people ask

How do you write Randy in Korean?

Randy is written 랜디 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: raendi). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has two syllables: 랜 + 디.

How do you pronounce Randy in Korean?

Say it raen-di, with even weight on every syllable. The opening ㄹ is a single light tap, halfway between an English r and l. Never roll it.
