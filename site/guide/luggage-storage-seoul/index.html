Luggage storage in Seoul: lockers, delivery and city check-in

Checked out at 11 but flying at 9pm? Here's where to leave your bags, what it costs in 2026, and how to send them straight to the airport.

3 min readChecked 18 October 2026
At a glance1 / 6
The short version
  • Quick stop: T-Locker subway lockers in 273 stations, from ₩2,200 for 4 hours (small, weekday). A large one fits a suitcase.
  • Staffed and bigger bags: T-Luggage counters at Seoul Station, Myeongdong and Hongik Univ., 9am–10pm, from ₩3,000.
  • Flying out later: send bags from a station or your hotel to Incheon from about ₩20,000–30,000. Book a day ahead.
  • Flying Korean airlines: check in and drop bags at Seoul Station's City Airport Terminal with a same-day AREX Express ticket.

Subway lockers (T-Locker)

Seoul Metro runs smart lockers in 273 stations, open 5am to midnight. They come in three sizes:

SizeFitsFirst 4 hours (weekday / weekend)Each extra hour
SmallBackpack, shopping bags₩2,200 / ₩3,100+₩500
MediumCarry-on₩3,300 / ₩4,600+₩800
LargeBig suitcase₩4,400 / ₩6,100+₩1,000

Pay by credit card at the locker or in the T-Locker app (또타라커), which also shows which lockers are free before you get there. Some foreign cards get rejected, so have a backup card. Older guides quote ₩2,000–4,000; those prices are out of date. Don't leave things for days: long-stay items are moved and eventually disposed of.

Staffed counters (T-Luggage)

For bigger or more bags, or if the lockers are full, Seoul Metro's staffed T-Luggage counters keep them for you from 9am to 10pm. Find them at Seoul Station (B1, Exit 1), Myeongdong (B1, Exits 9–10), Hongik Univ. (B1, Exits 3–4), Jamsil, Suseo, Jongno 3-ga and Gimpo Airport Station. Prices start at ₩3,000 for 4 hours for a small bag on weekdays. Book at tluggage.co.kr (English) or call 1877-1265.

Apps like Bounce, Stasher and Radical Storage also partner with shops around Seoul, usually from about ₩5,000–8,000 a day per bag.

Send your bags to the airport

The best trick for a late flight: check out, hand over your bags, spend the day hands-free and collect them at Incheon.

  • From a station: T-Luggage delivers to Incheon from ₩20,000 for a small or medium bag on weekdays. Drop off in the morning.
  • From your hotel: services like Seoul PASS, Trazy or Klook's T-Delivery collect from hotels with a staffed front desk, from about ₩25,000–30,000 a bag. Pickup cut-offs are usually 10–11am, and you need to book at least a day ahead.

Arriving? The same works in reverse. Counters at Incheon (Hanjin, in both terminals) can send your bags to your hotel while you head into town light.

Check in at Seoul Station

The City Airport Terminal on B2 of Seoul Station lets you check in, drop bags and clear immigration before you even get to the airport. It was renovated in August 2026. Check-in runs 5:20am–7pm and closes 3 hours before departure (3h20m for Terminal 2). You need a same-day AREX Express ticket, not the all-stop train. Airlines include Korean Air, Asiana, Jeju Air, T'way, Air Seoul, Air Busan, Jin Air and Eastar Jet. Some flights to the Americas are excluded, so check with your airline.

Nunchi tip

Staying in Hongdae, Myeongdong or near COEX and flying Korean Air, Asiana, Jeju Air or T'way? Look up Incheon Airport's "Easy Drop" counters: bag drop in the city, plus a fast-track lane at airport security.

Busan and the KTX

Busan Station added 150 extra-large lockers in July 2026, on top of hundreds of regular ones. Busan subway stations, including Haeundae, have lockers too. On the KTX, the rule is two bags per person that don't block the aisle or take a seat. Overhead racks fit carry-ons; big suitcases go in the space at the end of the car, which fills up fast on busy trains.

Questions people ask

How much are subway lockers in Seoul?

T-Locker prices start at ₩2,200 for 4 hours for a small locker on weekdays (₩3,100 at weekends). A large locker that fits a suitcase is ₩4,400 for 4 hours on weekdays.

Where can I store luggage at Seoul Station, Myeongdong or Hongdae?

Seoul Metro's staffed T-Luggage counters are at Seoul Station, Myeongdong and Hongik Univ. stations, open 9am–10pm, from ₩3,000 for 4 hours. All three stations also have lockers.

Can I send my luggage to Incheon Airport?

Yes. T-Luggage sends bags from stations from about ₩20,000, and hotel pickup services charge about ₩25,000–30,000 a bag. Drop off in the morning and book a day ahead.

Can I check in for my flight in central Seoul?

Yes, at the City Airport Terminal at Seoul Station, with a same-day AREX Express ticket, for Korean Air, Asiana, Jeju Air, T'way and several other airlines.
