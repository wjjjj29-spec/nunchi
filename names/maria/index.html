Maria in Korean 마리아

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Maria as 마리아: three syllables, said ma-ri-a. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Pronunciation tip

ㄹ between two vowels (마리) is one quick tap of the tongue, like the tt in American "butter": closer to a soft d than an English r.

  • ㅇ at the top of 아 is silent, so 아 is just the vowel: a.
  • Give all 3 syllables the same weight. Korean has no strong stress, so it's ma-ri-a, evenly.

How Koreans would call you

Friends calling you over
마리아야!
마리아 ends in a vowel, so it takes 야. Casual, for close friends.
At work or with new people
마리아 씨
마리아 ssi. Polite, roughly "Mr" or "Ms", but used with the first name.
Customer, client, online
마리아 님
마리아 nim. What you'll see on a delivery box or a booking.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables (after a one-syllable family name), so at 3 syllables 마리아 reads as a foreign name at a glance. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 마리아 or 마리아 씨.

Why it's spelled 마리아

Here the letters and the sound line up: applying the loanword rules letter by letter already gives 마리아, the same spelling Korean media use.

Questions people ask

How do you write Maria in Korean?

Maria is written 마리아 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: maria). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has three syllables: 마 + 리 + 아.

How do you pronounce Maria in Korean?

Say it ma-ri-a, with even weight on every syllable. ㄹ between two vowels (마리) is one quick tap of the tongue, like the tt in American "butter": closer to a soft d than an English r.
