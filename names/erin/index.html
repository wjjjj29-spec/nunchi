Erin in Korean 에린

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Erin as 에린: two syllables, said e-rin. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Pronunciation tip

ㄹ between two vowels (에린) is one quick tap of the tongue, like the tt in American "butter": closer to a soft d than an English r.

  • Two even beats, e-rin: Korean doesn't stress one syllable over the other the way English does.

에린 nim. What you'll see on a delivery box or a booking.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables, so 에린 already sounds natural to Korean ears. In casual sentences Koreans often add 이 after a name that ends in a consonant: 에린이 왔어 ("Erin's here").

Why it's spelled 에린

Questions people ask

How do you write Erin in Korean?

Erin is written 에린 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: erin). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has two syllables: 에 + 린.

How do you pronounce Erin in Korean?

Say it e-rin, with even weight on every syllable. ㄹ between two vowels (에린) is one quick tap of the tongue, like the tt in American "butter": closer to a soft d than an English r.
