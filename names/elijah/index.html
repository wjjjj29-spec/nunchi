Elijah in Korean 일라이자

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Elijah as 일라이자: four syllables, said il-la-i-ja. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Pronunciation tip

The double ㄹ in 일라 is a clear English l, as in "hello", not an r.

  • ㅇ at the top of 이 is silent, so 이 is just the vowel: i.
  • Give all 4 syllables the same weight. Korean has no strong stress, so it's il-la-i-ja, evenly.

일라이자 씨
일라이자 ssi. Polite, roughly "Mr" or "Ms", but used with the first name.
Customer, client, online
일라이자 님
일라이자 nim. What you'll see on a delivery box or a booking.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables (after a one-syllable family name), so at 4 syllables 일라이자 reads as a foreign name at a glance. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 일라이자 or 일라이자 씨.

Why it's spelled 일라이자

How Koreans write it
일라이자
the settled media spelling

Spelled letter-by-letter you'd get 엘리자, but Korean media write 일라이자, because the name is written the way it sounds, not the way it's spelled.

  • The final h is silent and is dropped.
  • The long i (as in "my") is spelled 아이: two beats in Korean, one in English.
  • An l between vowels is written twice (final ㄹ + ㄹ), so Koreans say a clear l instead of a tapped r.

Questions people ask

How do you write Elijah in Korean?

Elijah is written 일라이자 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: illaija). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has four syllables: 일 + 라 + 이 + 자.

How do you pronounce Elijah in Korean?

Say it il-la-i-ja, with even weight on every syllable. The double ㄹ in 일라 is a clear English l, as in "hello", not an r.
