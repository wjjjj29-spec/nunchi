Laura in Korean 로라

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Laura as 로라: two syllables, said ro-ra. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Pronunciation tip

ㄹ between two vowels (로라) is one quick tap of the tongue, like the tt in American "butter": closer to a soft d than an English r.

  • The opening ㄹ is a single light tap, halfway between an English r and l. Never roll it.
  • ㅗ is a pure "o" with rounded lips. Don't add the w-glide English puts in "go".
  • Two even beats, ro-ra: Korean doesn't stress one syllable over the other the way English does.

How Koreans would call you

Friends calling you over
로라야!
로라 ends in a vowel, so it takes 야. Casual, for close friends.
At work or with new people
로라 씨
로라 ssi. Polite, roughly "Mr" or "Ms", but used with the first name.
Customer, client, online
로라 님
로라 nim. What you'll see on a delivery box or a booking.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables, so 로라 already sounds natural to Korean ears. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 로라 or 로라 씨.

Why it's spelled 로라

Here the letters and the sound line up: applying the loanword rules letter by letter already gives 로라, the same spelling Korean media use.

Questions people ask

How do you write Laura in Korean?

Laura is written 로라 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: rora). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has two syllables: 로 + 라.

How do you pronounce Laura in Korean?

Say it ro-ra, with even weight on every syllable. ㄹ between two vowels (로라) is one quick tap of the tongue, like the tt in American "butter": closer to a soft d than an English r.
