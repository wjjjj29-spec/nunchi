Kartik in Korean 카르티크

Pronunciation tip

ㄹ between two vowels (카르) is one quick tap of the tongue, like the tt in American "butter": closer to a soft d than an English r.

  • 르 and 크 carry the filler vowel ㅡ (eu). Keep them short and light, almost just the consonant.
  • ㅋ at the start is aspirated: a clear puff of air, like the k in "kite".
  • Give all 4 syllables the same weight. Korean has no strong stress, so it's ka-reu-ti-keu, evenly.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables (after a one-syllable family name), so at 4 syllables 카르티크 reads as a foreign name at a glance. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 카르티크 or 카르티크 씨.

Spelled letter-by-letter you'd get 카틱, but Korean media write 카르티크, because the name is written the way it sounds, not the way it's spelled.

  • Korean adds the vowel ㅡ to consonants that have no vowel of their own (르 and 크), which is why Kartik has 4 syllables in Korean.

Questions people ask

How do you write Kartik in Korean?

Kartik is written 카르티크 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: kareutikeu). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has four syllables: 카 + 르 + 티 + 크.

How do you pronounce Kartik in Korean?

Say it ka-reu-ti-keu, with even weight on every syllable. ㄹ between two vowels (카르) is one quick tap of the tongue, like the tt in American "butter": closer to a soft d than an English r.

What does Kartik mean in Korean?

On its own, nothing: 카르티크 spells the sound of Kartik, so the syllables are not Korean words and Koreans read it simply as a foreign name.
