Reed in Korean 리드

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Reed as 리드: two syllables, said ri-deu. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Reed in Hangul
리드
Revised Romanization: rideu · said ri-deu · two syllables

Reed letter by letter

Each Hangul block is one syllable, built from a consonant, a vowel and sometimes a final consonant underneath.

Pronunciation tip

The opening ㄹ is a single light tap, halfway between an English r and l. Never roll it.

  • 드 carries the filler vowel ㅡ (eu). Keep it short and light, almost just the consonant.
  • Two even beats, ri-deu: Korean doesn't stress one syllable over the other the way English does.

How Koreans would call you

Friends calling you over
리드야!
리드 ends in a vowel, so it takes 야. Casual, for close friends.
At work or with new people
리드 씨
리드 ssi. Polite, roughly "Mr" or "Ms", but used with the first name.
Customer, client, online
리드 님
리드 nim. What you'll see on a delivery box or a booking.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables, so 리드 already sounds natural to Korean ears. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 리드 or 리드 씨.

Why it's spelled 리드

Here the letters and the sound line up: applying the loanword rules letter by letter already gives 리드, the same spelling Korean media use.

  • Korean adds the vowel ㅡ to consonants that have no vowel of their own (드), which is why Reed has 2 syllables in Korean.

Questions people ask

How do you write Reed in Korean?

Reed is written 리드 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: rideu). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has two syllables: 리 + 드.

How do you pronounce Reed in Korean?

Say it ri-deu, with even weight on every syllable. The opening ㄹ is a single light tap, halfway between an English r and l. Never roll it.
