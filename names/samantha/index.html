Samantha in Korean 서맨사

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Samantha as 서맨사: three syllables, said seo-maen-sa. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Pronunciation tip

ㅓ (eo) in 서 is the open "uh" of "sun", not an "o".

  • ㅐ (ae) is said like the e in "bed" by most Koreans today, a little more closed than the a in "cat".
  • Give all 3 syllables the same weight. Korean has no strong stress, so it's seo-maen-sa, evenly.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables (after a one-syllable family name), so at 3 syllables 서맨사 reads as a foreign name at a glance. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 서맨사 or 서맨사 씨.

Spelled letter-by-letter you'd get 사만사, but Korean media write 서맨사, because the name is written the way it sounds, not the way it's spelled.

  • Korean has no th, so th becomes ㅅ (s), the closest sound.
  • The unstressed "uh" vowel is written ㅓ (eo), whatever letter English uses for it.

Questions people ask

How do you write Samantha in Korean?

Samantha is written 서맨사 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: seomaensa). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has three syllables: 서 + 맨 + 사.

How do you pronounce Samantha in Korean?

Say it seo-maen-sa, with even weight on every syllable. ㅓ (eo) in 서 is the open "uh" of "sun", not an "o".
