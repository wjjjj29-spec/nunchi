Alessandro in Korean 알레산드로

Pronunciation tip

ㄹ between two vowels (드로) is one quick tap of the tongue, like the tt in American "butter": closer to a soft d than an English r.

  • The double ㄹ in 알레 is a clear English l, as in "hello", not an r.
  • 드 carries the filler vowel ㅡ (eu). Keep it short and light, almost just the consonant.
  • ㅗ is a pure "o" with rounded lips. Don't add the w-glide English puts in "go".
  • Give all 5 syllables the same weight. Korean has no strong stress, so it's al-le-san-deu-ro, evenly.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables (after a one-syllable family name), so at 5 syllables 알레산드로 reads as a foreign name at a glance. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 알레산드로 or 알레산드로 씨.

Why it's spelled 알레산드로

Here the letters and the sound line up: applying the loanword rules letter by letter already gives 알레산드로, the same spelling Korean media use.

  • Korean follows the Italian sound: every vowel is pronounced and double consonants are written once.

Questions people ask

How do you write Alessandro in Korean?

How do you pronounce Alessandro in Korean?

Say it al-le-san-deu-ro, with even weight on every syllable. ㄹ between two vowels (드로) is one quick tap of the tongue, like the tt in American "butter": closer to a soft d than an English r.
