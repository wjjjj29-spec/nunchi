Which age do Koreans actually use?

Since June 2023, laws and official documents use international age, so your passport age is your legal age in Korea too. But people still ask "몇 년생이에요?" (what year were you born?) instead of "how old are you?". Answer 92년생이에요 and everyone knows exactly where you fit, because in Korea the birth year, not the birthday, decides who is older.

Year age (34 in 2026) still decides when you can buy alcohol and cigarettes (from year age 19) and when men are called up for military service.

The 92-year-olds at school

Korean children born in 1992 started elementary school in March 1999 and, if they went straight to university, entered in 2011 as 11학번 (11 "hakbeon", the university entry year Koreans use as a second age label).

빠른년생: the January–February exception

Until 2009, children born in January and February started school a year early with the previous year's class. So someone born in early 1992 may call themselves a 91년생 and be friends, not juniors, with people born in 1991. It's called 빠른년생 (ppareun nyeonsaeng) and it still confuses Korean friend groups.

Who's older: oppa, unnie, hyung or noona?

Anyone born before 1992 is older than you in Korea, even if their birthday is in December and yours is in January. Someone born in 1992 too is your 동갑 (same age), and you can drop the honorifics.

Questions people ask

How old is someone born in 1992 in Korean age?

35 in 2026. Korean age is the current year minus the birth year, plus one.

What is the international age of someone born in 1992?

33 until their birthday in 2026, then 34.

What zodiac animal is 1992?

The Monkey (원숭이띠). If you were born in January or early February, before Lunar New Year, Koreans count you as the Goat (양띠) instead.
