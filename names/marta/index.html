Marta in Korean 마르타

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Marta as 마르타: three syllables, said ma-reu-ta. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Pronunciation tip

ㄹ between two vowels (마르) is one quick tap of the tongue, like the tt in American "butter": closer to a soft d than an English r.

  • 르 carries the filler vowel ㅡ (eu). Keep it short and light, almost just the consonant.
  • Give all 3 syllables the same weight. Korean has no strong stress, so it's ma-reu-ta, evenly.

마르타 nim. What you'll see on a delivery box or a booking.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables (after a one-syllable family name), so at 3 syllables 마르타 reads as a foreign name at a glance. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 마르타 or 마르타 씨.

How Koreans write it
마르타
the settled media spelling

Spelled letter-by-letter you'd get 마타, but Korean media write 마르타, because the name is written the way it sounds in Spanish, not the way it's spelled.

  • Korean writes Spanish names by their Spanish sound: vowels are read as written and the r is pronounced (ㄹ/르) instead of being dropped as in English.

Questions people ask

How do you write Marta in Korean?

Marta is written 마르타 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: mareuta). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name and follows the Spanish pronunciation. In Hangul it has three syllables: 마 + 르 + 타.

How do you pronounce Marta in Korean?

Say it ma-reu-ta, with even weight on every syllable. ㄹ between two vowels (마르) is one quick tap of the tongue, like the tt in American "butter": closer to a soft d than an English r.
