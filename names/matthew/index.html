Matthew in Korean 매슈

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Matthew as 매슈: two syllables, said mae-syu. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Pronunciation tip

ㅐ (ae) is said like the e in "bed" by most Koreans today, a little more closed than the a in "cat".

  • Two even beats, mae-syu: Korean doesn't stress one syllable over the other the way English does.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables, so 매슈 already sounds natural to Korean ears. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 매슈 or 매슈 씨.

Why it's spelled 매슈

How Koreans write it
매슈
the settled media spelling

Spelled letter-by-letter you'd get 매트휴, but Korean media write 매슈, because the name is written the way it sounds, not the way it's spelled.

  • Korean has no th, so th becomes ㅅ (s), the closest sound.
  • Double letters count once. Hangul spells the sound, not the letters.
