Josh in Korean 조시

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Josh as 조시: two syllables, said jo-si. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Josh in Hangul
조시
Revised Romanization: josi · said jo-si · two syllables

Josh letter by letter

Each Hangul block is one syllable, built from a consonant, a vowel and sometimes a final consonant underneath.

조
ㅈ j + ㅗ o
jo
시
ㅅ s + ㅣ i
si
Pronunciation tip

The final 시 stands for the English "sh" sound. Say the i very lightly.

  • ㅈ at the start is soft and unvoiced, halfway between ch and j.
  • ㅗ is a pure "o" with rounded lips. Don't add the w-glide English puts in "go".
  • Two even beats, jo-si: Korean doesn't stress one syllable over the other the way English does.

How Koreans would call you

Friends calling you over
조시야!
조시 ends in a vowel, so it takes 야. Casual, for close friends.
At work or with new people
조시 씨
조시 ssi. Polite, roughly "Mr" or "Ms", but used with the first name.
Customer, client, online
조시 님
조시 nim. What you'll see on a delivery box or a booking.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables, so 조시 already sounds natural to Korean ears. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 조시 or 조시 씨.

Why it's spelled 조시

Here the letters and the sound line up: applying the loanword rules letter by letter already gives 조시, the same spelling Korean media use.

  • A final "sh" sound gets the vowel ㅣ (시) rather than ㅡ.

Questions people ask

How do you write Josh in Korean?

Josh is written 조시 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: josi). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has two syllables: 조 + 시.

How do you pronounce Josh in Korean?

Say it jo-si, with even weight on every syllable. The final 시 stands for the English "sh" sound. Say the i very lightly.
