Seoul weather in November and December: what to expect and pack

Crisp autumn turns into dry, windy winter fast. Average temperatures, first snow, fine dust and how Koreans dress for the cold.

4 min readChecked 20 October 2026
At a glance1 / 6
The short version
  • November: about 12°C by day and 3.5°C at night. Crisp and often clear, with first frost early in the month and the first flakes of snow around 20 November.
  • December: about 4°C by day and −3°C at night, with a dry, sharp wind that makes it feel colder than it reads.
  • Pack: a warm padded coat, thermal base layers, hat and gloves. Buildings and trains are heated, so layer rather than bulk up.
  • Air: fine dust picks up from late autumn. Check AirKorea or Misemise each morning and wear a KF94 mask on bad days.

Pack for cold, not for snow. November in Seoul goes from crisp autumn to early winter, with average highs of about 12°C and lows of about 3.5°C. December is properly cold: highs of about 4°C, lows of about −3°C and a dry, biting wind. Indoors is the opposite. Subways, shops and homes are heated hard, so bring layers you can take off.

These are the Korea Meteorological Administration's (KMA) 1991–2020 averages. "Days with rain or snow" counts days with at least 1mm; "days with any snow" includes flurries and sleet. Averages hide cold snaps: on 4 December 2025, Seoul woke up to −9.2°C.

First frost, first snow and the wind

  • First frost and ice: normally early November. In 2025 Seoul saw both on 28 October, about six days earlier than normal.
  • First snow: on average around 20 November. KMA counts any snow, including sleet, so the first one is often just a few flakes.
  • Wind: December's dry northwesterly wind makes it feel much colder than the number. At 0°C with a 20km/h wind it feels like about −5°C. Check the "feels like" temperature (체감온도, chegam ondo) in your weather app, not just the headline figure.

Autumn leaves end early

The leaves came early in 2026. KMA recorded first colour on Bukhansan, the mountain on Seoul's northern edge, on 14 October, and the peak was estimated for around 27–28 October. Arrive in early November and you'll catch the last of it in Seoul. Colour moves south later, so the southern provinces are still turning into November.

Fine dust and yellow dust

Fine dust (미세먼지, misemeonji) is worst from late autumn to spring. The government runs a special fine-dust season every December to March, cutting coal power and limiting the oldest, most polluting cars in the capital region. Yellow dust (황사, hwangsa), blown in from the deserts of China and Mongolia, is mainly a spring problem, but it occasionally arrives in late autumn.

Check the air each morning on AirKorea, the government's monitoring service, which has an English site, or the Korean app 미세미세 (Misemise), which shows the level as a coloured face. On bad days, wear a KF94 mask (sold in convenience stores and pharmacies) and plan indoor sights. Our pharmacy guide has the words for a sore throat and cough.

Heating: layer, don't bulk up

Korean buildings run warm. Homes and many guesthouses have 온돌 (ondol) underfloor heating, and subways, shops, cafés and restaurants are heated too. You'll go from a freezing street to a warm, packed train carriage in a minute. Thin layers under one warm coat you can unzip work better than a thick jumper you can't take off.

What Koreans wear

  • A padded coat. Once the cold sets in, the city switches to puffer jackets, and the knee-length 롱패딩 (rong paeding, "long padding") is everywhere.
  • Thermal underwear. Uniqlo's Heattech or similar base layers under normal clothes. In Korean these are 발열내의 (baryeol naeui).
  • Hand warmers. Every convenience store sells disposable 핫팩 (hatpaek), usually for a couple of thousand won or less and often on 1+1 deals.
  • The rest: hat, gloves, scarf, warm socks, and shoes with grip for icy pavements in December.
Nunchi tip

Unzip your coat before you go down to the subway. Stations and trains are heated, and a sealed puffer in a packed carriage is the fastest way to overheat.

Short days

On 1 November the sun rises at about 6:55am and sets at about 5:36pm. By the winter solstice it's 7:41am to 5:18pm, under 10 hours of daylight. Do palaces, hikes and outdoor markets in the morning and save shopping, food streets and night views for after dark.

Christmas lights and the end of the year

Christmas Day is a public holiday in Korea. In 2026 it falls on a Friday, so expect a busy long weekend; see the full list on our public holidays calendar. Seoul's city-run Seoul Winter Festa last ran from 12 December 2025 to 4 January 2026, with a Christmas market at Gwanghwamun Square, a lantern festival along Cheonggyecheon Stream, an ice rink at Seoul Plaza and the New Year's Eve bell at Bosingak. The 2026 dates hadn't been announced by 20 October, so check Seoul city's website closer to the time.

Busan is about 5 degrees milder than Seoul and averages less than one snowy day in December. Jeju City is milder still at night but greyer: in December it gets some rain or drizzle on about 11 days and about half of Seoul's sunshine. Bring a windproof layer for the coast in both.

Questions people ask

How cold is Seoul in November?

Average highs are about 12°C and lows about 3.5°C, based on KMA's 1991–2020 averages. Early November can still feel like autumn; by the end of the month mornings are close to freezing.

Does it snow in Seoul in December?

Usually, a little. Seoul averages about 6 days with some snow in December, counting flurries and sleet. The first snow typically falls around 20 November.

What should I wear in Seoul in December?

A warm padded coat, thermal base layers, a hat, gloves and a scarf. Lows average about −3°C and the wind makes it feel colder, but indoors is heated, so wear layers you can take off.

Will there still be autumn leaves in November 2026?

Only the tail end in Seoul. Bukhansan's colour started on 14 October 2026 and was estimated to peak around 27–28 October. The southern provinces turn later.

Is Busan warmer than Seoul in winter?

Yes. Busan's average December high is about 10°C and its low about 2°C, against 4°C and −3°C in Seoul, and it rarely snows.
