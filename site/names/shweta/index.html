Shweta in Korean 슈웨타

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Shweta as 슈웨타: three syllables, said syu-we-ta. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Pronunciation tip

ㅞ in 웨 is one gliding vowel: we, in a single beat.

  • ㅇ at the top of 웨 is silent, so 웨 is just the vowel: we.
  • Give all 3 syllables the same weight. Korean has no strong stress, so it's syu-we-ta, evenly.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables (after a one-syllable family name), so at 3 syllables 슈웨타 reads as a foreign name at a glance. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 슈웨타 or 슈웨타 씨.

Questions people ask

How do you write Shweta in Korean?

Shweta is written 슈웨타 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: syuweta). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has three syllables: 슈 + 웨 + 타.

How do you pronounce Shweta in Korean?

Say it syu-we-ta, with even weight on every syllable. ㅞ in 웨 is one gliding vowel: we, in a single beat.

What does Shweta mean in Korean?

On its own, nothing: 슈웨타 spells the sound of Shweta, so the syllables are not Korean words and Koreans read it simply as a foreign name.
