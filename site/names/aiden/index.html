Aiden in Korean 에이든

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Aiden as 에이든: three syllables, said e-i-deun. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Pronunciation tip

든 carries the filler vowel ㅡ (eu). Keep it short and light, almost just the consonant.

  • ㅇ at the top of 이 is silent, so 이 is just the vowel: i.
  • Give all 3 syllables the same weight. Korean has no strong stress, so it's e-i-deun, evenly.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables (after a one-syllable family name), so at 3 syllables 에이든 reads as a foreign name at a glance. In casual sentences Koreans often add 이 after a name that ends in a consonant: 에이든이 왔어 ("Aiden's here").

Why it's spelled 에이든

How Koreans write it
에이든
the settled media spelling

Spelled letter-by-letter you'd get 에이덴, but Korean media write 에이든, because the name is written the way it sounds, not the way it's spelled.

  • The long a (as in "day") is spelled as two beats, 에이, because Korean writes each part of the diphthong.
  • Korean adds the vowel ㅡ to consonants that have no vowel of their own (든), which is why Aiden has 3 syllables in Korean.

Questions people ask

How do you write Aiden in Korean?

Aiden is written 에이든 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: eideun). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has three syllables: 에 + 이 + 든.

How do you pronounce Aiden in Korean?

Say it e-i-deun, with even weight on every syllable. 든 carries the filler vowel ㅡ (eu). Keep it short and light, almost just the consonant.
