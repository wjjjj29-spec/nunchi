Payal in Korean 파얄

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Payal as 파얄: two syllables, said pa-yal. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Payal letter by letter

Each Hangul block is one syllable, built from a consonant, a vowel and sometimes a final consonant underneath.

파
ㅍ p + ㅏ a
pa
얄
ㅇ silent + ㅑ ya + ㄹ l (final)

Pronunciation tip

ㄹ at the bottom of 얄 is an l: the tongue touches just behind the teeth and stays there.

  • ㅍ at the start is aspirated: a clear puff of air, like the p in "pie".
  • ㅇ at the top of 얄 is silent, so 얄 is just the vowel: yal.
  • Two even beats, pa-yal: Korean doesn't stress one syllable over the other the way English does.

Questions people ask

How do you write Payal in Korean?

Payal is written 파얄 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: payal). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has two syllables: 파 + 얄.

How do you pronounce Payal in Korean?

Say it pa-yal, with even weight on every syllable. ㄹ at the bottom of 얄 is an l: the tongue touches just behind the teeth and stays there.

What does Payal mean in Korean?

On its own, nothing: 파얄 spells the sound of Payal, so the syllables are not Korean words and Koreans read it simply as a foreign name.
