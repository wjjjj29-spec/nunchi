Why a Korean account matters (and what we do instead)

In 2026 the big Korean ticket sites started giving Korean-verified accounts priority in some sales, and the Korean versions of Melon Ticket and Interpark need a Korean phone number and ID to even sign up. Overseas fans get the global sites, which open later and sometimes carry fewer seats. We can't change that, and we won't buy under a Korean account for you: those tickets are ID-checked at the door and get cancelled. What we can do is make sure you're never late, never on the wrong site, and never surprised by a pre-sale you didn't know existed.

Questions people ask

Can foreigners buy K-pop concert tickets in Korea?

Yes, through the global versions of the ticket sites: NOL World (formerly Interpark Global), Yes24 Global and Melon Ticket's global sale. The Korean sites need a Korean phone number and ID. Our alerts tell you which site sells each show to overseas fans and when.

Do you buy tickets for me?

No. Buying under someone else's account breaks the sites' rules and tickets are ID-checked at the door. We tell you where and when to buy, and how that site's queue works, so you can do it yourself.

Which artists do you cover?

Any artist with a ticketed event in Korea. You pick up to 10, and you can add or change them anytime by replying to any alert.

How fast are the alerts?

We check the ticket sites every hour. Most on-sales are announced a week or more ahead, so you'll usually have days to prepare, not minutes.

What if I'm not going to Korea?

Then you probably don't need this. We only cover events in Korea. For world tours, the artist's agency announces local on-sales directly.
