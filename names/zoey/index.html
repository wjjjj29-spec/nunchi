Zoey in Korean 조이

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Zoey as 조이: two syllables, said jo-i. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Pronunciation tip

ㅈ at the start is soft and unvoiced, halfway between ch and j.

  • ㅗ is a pure "o" with rounded lips. Don't add the w-glide English puts in "go".
  • ㅇ at the top of 이 is silent, so 이 is just the vowel: i.
  • Two even beats, jo-i: Korean doesn't stress one syllable over the other the way English does.

How Koreans would call you

Friends calling you over
조이야!
조이 ends in a vowel, so it takes 야. Casual, for close friends.
At work or with new people
조이 씨
조이 ssi. Polite, roughly "Mr" or "Ms", but used with the first name.
Customer, client, online
조이 님
조이 nim. What you'll see on a delivery box or a booking.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables, so 조이 already sounds natural to Korean ears. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 조이 or 조이 씨.

Why it's spelled 조이

Here the letters and the sound line up: applying the loanword rules letter by letter already gives 조이, the same spelling Korean media use.

  • Korean has no z, so z becomes ㅈ (j).

Questions people ask

How do you write Zoey in Korean?

Zoey is written 조이 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: joi). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has two syllables: 조 + 이.

How do you pronounce Zoey in Korean?

Say it jo-i, with even weight on every syllable. ㅈ at the start is soft and unvoiced, halfway between ch and j.
