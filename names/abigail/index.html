Abigail in Korean 애비게일

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Abigail as 애비게일: four syllables, said ae-bi-ge-il. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Pronunciation tip

ㄹ at the bottom of 일 is an l: the tongue touches just behind the teeth and stays there.

  • ㅐ and ㅔ sound the same in modern Seoul speech: the e in "bed".
  • ㅇ at the top of 일 is silent, so 일 is just the vowel: il.
  • Give all 4 syllables the same weight. Korean has no strong stress, so it's ae-bi-ge-il, evenly.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables (after a one-syllable family name), so at 4 syllables 애비게일 reads as a foreign name at a glance. In casual sentences Koreans often add 이 after a name that ends in a consonant: 애비게일이 왔어 ("Abigail's here").

Questions people ask

How do you write Abigail in Korean?

Abigail is written 애비게일 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: aebigeil). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has four syllables: 애 + 비 + 게 + 일.

How do you pronounce Abigail in Korean?

Say it ae-bi-ge-il, with even weight on every syllable. ㄹ at the bottom of 일 is an l: the tongue touches just behind the teeth and stays there.
