제목: I built a free converter that writes your name in Hangul the way Korean media actually does (not letter-by-letter) — tell me which names it gets wrong
본문:
Most tools transliterate by spelling, so Siobhan turns into 시오브한 and Michael into 미카엘. Korean newspapers have settled spellings (마이클, 셔본) and the National Institute of Korean Language has loanword rules for the rest.
I put both into one page: hellonunchi.com/tools/my-name-in-hangul/
It has ~550 names in the dictionary and falls back to the rules. It also shows how friends would call you (야/아) and the polite 씨 form.
It's definitely wrong on some names — post yours and I'll fix the dictionary today.
