Syafiq in Korean 샤피크

Pronunciation tip

크 carries the filler vowel ㅡ (eu). Keep it short and light, almost just the consonant.

  • Give all 3 syllables the same weight. Korean has no strong stress, so it's sya-pi-keu, evenly.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables (after a one-syllable family name), so at 3 syllables 샤피크 reads as a foreign name at a glance. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 샤피크 or 샤피크 씨.

Why it's spelled 샤피크

Spelled letter-by-letter you'd get 스야픽, but Korean media write 샤피크, because the name is written the way it sounds, not the way it's spelled.

  • Korean has no f sound, so f becomes ㅍ (p), said with a puff of air.
  • Korean adds the vowel ㅡ to consonants that have no vowel of their own (크), which is why Syafiq has 3 syllables in Korean.

Questions people ask

How do you write Syafiq in Korean?

Syafiq is written 샤피크 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: syapikeu). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has three syllables: 샤 + 피 + 크.

How do you pronounce Syafiq in Korean?

Say it sya-pi-keu, with even weight on every syllable. 크 carries the filler vowel ㅡ (eu). Keep it short and light, almost just the consonant.

What does Syafiq mean in Korean?

On its own, nothing: 샤피크 spells the sound of Syafiq, so the syllables are not Korean words and Koreans read it simply as a foreign name.
