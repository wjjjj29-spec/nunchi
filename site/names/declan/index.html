Declan in Korean 데클런

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Declan as 데클런: three syllables, said de-keul-leon. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Pronunciation tip

The double ㄹ in 클런 is a clear English l, as in "hello", not an r.

  • 클 carries the filler vowel ㅡ (eu). Keep it short and light, almost just the consonant.
  • ㅓ (eo) in 런 is the open "uh" of "sun", not an "o".
  • ㄷ at the start is soft and unvoiced, halfway between t and d.
  • Give all 3 syllables the same weight. Korean has no strong stress, so it's de-keul-leon, evenly.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables (after a one-syllable family name), so at 3 syllables 데클런 reads as a foreign name at a glance. In casual sentences Koreans often add 이 after a name that ends in a consonant: 데클런이 왔어 ("Declan's here").

Why it's spelled 데클런

How Koreans write it
데클런
the settled media spelling

Spelled letter-by-letter you'd get 데크랜, but Korean media write 데클런, because the name is written the way it sounds, not the way it's spelled.

  • An l between vowels is written twice (final ㄹ + ㄹ), so Koreans say a clear l instead of a tapped r.
  • The unstressed "uh" vowel is written ㅓ (eo), whatever letter English uses for it.
  • Korean adds the vowel ㅡ to consonants that have no vowel of their own (클), which is why Declan has 3 syllables in Korean.

Questions people ask

How do you write Declan in Korean?

Declan is written 데클런 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: dekeulleon). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has three syllables: 데 + 클 + 런.

How do you pronounce Declan in Korean?

Say it de-keul-leon, with even weight on every syllable. The double ㄹ in 클런 is a clear English l, as in "hello", not an r.
