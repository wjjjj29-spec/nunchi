Stephanie in Korean 스테퍼니

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Stephanie as 스테퍼니: four syllables, said seu-te-peo-ni. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Pronunciation tip

스 carries the filler vowel ㅡ (eu). Keep it short and light, almost just the consonant.

  • ㅓ (eo) in 퍼 is the open "uh" of "sun", not an "o".
  • Give all 4 syllables the same weight. Korean has no strong stress, so it's seu-te-peo-ni, evenly.

스테퍼니 nim. What you'll see on a delivery box or a booking.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables (after a one-syllable family name), so at 4 syllables 스테퍼니 reads as a foreign name at a glance. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 스테퍼니 or 스테퍼니 씨.

Why it's spelled 스테퍼니

How Koreans write it

the settled media spelling

Spelled letter-by-letter you'd get 스테파니, but Korean media write 스테퍼니, because the name is written the way it sounds, not the way it's spelled.

  • ph is an f sound, and Korean has no f, so it becomes ㅍ (p).
  • The unstressed "uh" vowel is written ㅓ (eo), whatever letter English uses for it.
  • Korean adds the vowel ㅡ to consonants that have no vowel of their own (스), which is why Stephanie has 4 syllables in Korean.

Questions people ask

How do you write Stephanie in Korean?

Stephanie is written 스테퍼니 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: seutepeoni). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has four syllables: 스 + 테 + 퍼 + 니.

How do you pronounce Stephanie in Korean?

Say it seu-te-peo-ni, with even weight on every syllable. 스 carries the filler vowel ㅡ (eu). Keep it short and light, almost just the consonant.
