Brandon in Korean 브랜던

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Brandon as 브랜던: three syllables, said beu-raen-deon. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Pronunciation tip

ㄹ between two vowels (브랜) is one quick tap of the tongue, like the tt in American "butter": closer to a soft d than an English r.

  • 브 carries the filler vowel ㅡ (eu). Keep it short and light, almost just the consonant.
  • ㅓ (eo) in 던 is the open "uh" of "sun", not an "o".
  • ㅐ (ae) is said like the e in "bed" by most Koreans today, a little more closed than the a in "cat".
  • ㅂ at the start is soft and unvoiced, halfway between p and b.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables (after a one-syllable family name), so at 3 syllables 브랜던 reads as a foreign name at a glance. In casual sentences Koreans often add 이 after a name that ends in a consonant: 브랜던이 왔어 ("Brandon's here").

Why it's spelled 브랜던

How Koreans write it

Spelled letter-by-letter you'd get 브랜돈, but Korean media write 브랜던, because the name is written the way it sounds, not the way it's spelled.

  • The unstressed "uh" vowel is written ㅓ (eo), whatever letter English uses for it.
  • Korean adds the vowel ㅡ to consonants that have no vowel of their own (브), which is why Brandon has 3 syllables in Korean.

Questions people ask

How do you write Brandon in Korean?

Brandon is written 브랜던 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: beuraendeon). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has three syllables: 브 + 랜 + 던.

How do you pronounce Brandon in Korean?

Say it beu-raen-deon, with even weight on every syllable. ㄹ between two vowels (브랜) is one quick tap of the tongue, like the tt in American "butter": closer to a soft d than an English r.
