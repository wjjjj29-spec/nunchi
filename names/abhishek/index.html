Pronunciation tip

크 carries the filler vowel ㅡ (eu). Keep it short and light, almost just the consonant.

  • Give all 4 syllables the same weight. Korean has no strong stress, so it's a-bi-sye-keu, evenly.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables (after a one-syllable family name), so at 4 syllables 아비셰크 reads as a foreign name at a glance. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 아비셰크 or 아비셰크 씨.

  • Korean adds the vowel ㅡ to consonants that have no vowel of their own (크), which is why Abhishek has 4 syllables in Korean.

Questions people ask

How do you write Abhishek in Korean?

Abhishek is written 아비셰크 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: abisyekeu). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has four syllables: 아 + 비 + 셰 + 크.

How do you pronounce Abhishek in Korean?

Say it a-bi-sye-keu, with even weight on every syllable. 크 carries the filler vowel ㅡ (eu). Keep it short and light, almost just the consonant.

What does Abhishek mean in Korean?

On its own, nothing: 아비셰크 spells the sound of Abhishek, so the syllables are not Korean words and Koreans read it simply as a foreign name.
