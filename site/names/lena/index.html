Lena in Korean 레나

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Lena as 레나: two syllables, said re-na. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Pronunciation tip

The opening ㄹ is a single light tap, halfway between an English r and l. Never roll it.

  • Two even beats, re-na: Korean doesn't stress one syllable over the other the way English does.

How Koreans would call you

At work or with new people

레나 ssi. Polite, roughly "Mr" or "Ms", but used with the first name.
Customer, client, online
레나 님
레나 nim. What you'll see on a delivery box or a booking.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables, so 레나 already sounds natural to Korean ears. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 레나 or 레나 씨.

Why it's spelled 레나

Here the letters and the sound line up: applying the loanword rules letter by letter already gives 레나, the same spelling Korean media use.

  • Korean follows the German sound: vowels are read as written.

Questions people ask

How do you write Lena in Korean?

Lena is written 레나 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: rena). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name and follows the German pronunciation. In Hangul it has two syllables: 레 + 나.

How do you pronounce Lena in Korean?

Say it re-na, with even weight on every syllable. The opening ㄹ is a single light tap, halfway between an English r and l. Never roll it.
