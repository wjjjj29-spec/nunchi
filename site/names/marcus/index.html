Marcus in Korean 마커스

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Marcus as 마커스: three syllables, said ma-keo-seu. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Pronunciation tip

스 carries the filler vowel ㅡ (eu). Keep it short and light, almost just the consonant.

  • ㅓ (eo) in 커 is the open "uh" of "sun", not an "o".
  • Give all 3 syllables the same weight. Korean has no strong stress, so it's ma-keo-seu, evenly.

At work or with new people

마커스 ssi. Polite, roughly "Mr" or "Ms", but used with the first name.
Customer, client, online
마커스 님
마커스 nim. What you'll see on a delivery box or a booking.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables (after a one-syllable family name), so at 3 syllables 마커스 reads as a foreign name at a glance. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 마커스 or 마커스 씨.

Why it's spelled 마커스

Here the letters and the sound line up: applying the loanword rules letter by letter already gives 마커스, the same spelling Korean media use.

  • An r after a vowel (before a consonant or at the end) isn't written. Korean spelling follows British-style pronunciation, where that r is silent.
  • The unstressed "uh" vowel is written ㅓ (eo), whatever letter English uses for it.
  • Korean adds the vowel ㅡ to consonants that have no vowel of their own (스), which is why Marcus has 3 syllables in Korean.

Questions people ask

How do you write Marcus in Korean?

Marcus is written 마커스 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: makeoseu). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has three syllables: 마 + 커 + 스.

How do you pronounce Marcus in Korean?

Say it ma-keo-seu, with even weight on every syllable. 스 carries the filler vowel ㅡ (eu). Keep it short and light, almost just the consonant.
