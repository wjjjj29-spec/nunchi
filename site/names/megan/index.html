Megan in Korean 메건

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Megan as 메건: two syllables, said me-geon. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Megan in Hangul

Megan letter by letter

Each Hangul block is one syllable, built from a consonant, a vowel and sometimes a final consonant underneath.

me
건
ㄱ g + ㅓ eo + ㄴ n (final)

Pronunciation tip

ㅓ (eo) in 건 is the open "uh" of "sun", not an "o".

  • Two even beats, me-geon: Korean doesn't stress one syllable over the other the way English does.

How Koreans would call you

Friends calling you over
메건아!
메건 ends in a consonant, so it takes 아, said 메거나. Casual, for close friends.
At work or with new people
메건 씨
메건 ssi. Polite, roughly "Mr" or "Ms", but used with the first name.
Customer, client, online
메건 님
메건 nim. What you'll see on a delivery box or a booking.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables, so 메건 already sounds natural to Korean ears. In casual sentences Koreans often add 이 after a name that ends in a consonant: 메건이 왔어 ("Megan's here").

How Koreans write it
메건
the settled media spelling

Spelled letter-by-letter you'd get 메갠, but Korean media write 메건, because the name is written the way it sounds, not the way it's spelled.

  • The unstressed "uh" vowel is written ㅓ (eo), whatever letter English uses for it.

Questions people ask

How do you write Megan in Korean?

Megan is written 메건 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: megeon). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has two syllables: 메 + 건.

How do you pronounce Megan in Korean?

Say it me-geon, with even weight on every syllable. ㅓ (eo) in 건 is the open "uh" of "sun", not an "o".
