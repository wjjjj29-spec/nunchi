Thiago in Korean 치아구

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Thiago as 치아구: three syllables, said chi-a-gu. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Pronunciation tip

ㅊ at the start is aspirated: a clear puff of air, like the ch in "church".

  • ㅇ at the top of 아 is silent, so 아 is just the vowel: a.
  • Give all 3 syllables the same weight. Korean has no strong stress, so it's chi-a-gu, evenly.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables (after a one-syllable family name), so at 3 syllables 치아구 reads as a foreign name at a glance. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 치아구 or 치아구 씨.

Spelled letter-by-letter you'd get 시아고, but Korean media write 치아구, because the name is written the way it sounds in Portuguese, not the way it's spelled.

  • Korean follows the Brazilian Portuguese sound: t before i sounds like 'ch' and a final o sounds like 'u'.

Questions people ask

How do you write Thiago in Korean?

Thiago is written 치아구 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: chiagu). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name and follows the Portuguese pronunciation. In Hangul it has three syllables: 치 + 아 + 구.

How do you pronounce Thiago in Korean?

Say it chi-a-gu, with even weight on every syllable. ㅊ at the start is aspirated: a clear puff of air, like the ch in "church".
