The Climate Card is a Seoul city product. It is excellent if you stay inside the city and ride a lot. Step outside Seoul on a day trip, or take the airport express, and the gate will refuse it. Most visitors who do both end up with a Climate Card for the city days and a T-money card for everything else, which is fine: the T-money card costs less than a coffee.

Questions people ask

Does the Climate Card work on the airport train?

No. Use T-money or buy an AREX ticket. The all-stop AREX train is about ₩4,000 to central Seoul; the express is ₩11,000.

Can I use my phone instead of a card?

Android phones with NFC can use mobile T-money apps. iPhone support in Korea is still limited, so most visitors carry the physical card.

Can I get money back from T-money?

Yes, at convenience stores for balances under ₩20,000, with a small fee. Or keep it; it does not expire and works on your next trip.
