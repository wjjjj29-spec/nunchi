Arlo in Korean 알로

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Arlo as 알로: two syllables, said al-lo. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Pronunciation tip

The double ㄹ in 알로 is a clear English l, as in "hello", not an r.

  • ㅗ is a pure "o" with rounded lips. Don't add the w-glide English puts in "go".
  • Two even beats, al-lo: Korean doesn't stress one syllable over the other the way English does.

Customer, client, online
알로 님
알로 nim. What you'll see on a delivery box or a booking.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables, so 알로 already sounds natural to Korean ears. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 알로 or 알로 씨.

Why it's spelled 알로

Here the letters and the sound line up: applying the loanword rules letter by letter already gives 알로, the same spelling Korean media use.

  • An r after a vowel (before a consonant or at the end) isn't written. Korean spelling follows British-style pronunciation, where that r is silent.
  • An l between vowels is written twice (final ㄹ + ㄹ), so Koreans say a clear l instead of a tapped r.

Questions people ask

How do you write Arlo in Korean?

Arlo is written 알로 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: allo). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has two syllables: 알 + 로.

How do you pronounce Arlo in Korean?

Say it al-lo, with even weight on every syllable. The double ㄹ in 알로 is a clear English l, as in "hello", not an r.
