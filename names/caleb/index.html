Pronunciation tip

The double ㄹ in 일럽 is a clear English l, as in "hello", not an r.

  • The final ㅂ in 럽 is unreleased: stop the sound (p) without a puff of air.
  • ㅓ (eo) in 럽 is the open "uh" of "sun", not an "o".
  • ㅋ at the start is aspirated: a clear puff of air, like the k in "kite".
  • ㅇ at the top of 일 is silent, so 일 is just the vowel: il.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables (after a one-syllable family name), so at 3 syllables 케일럽 reads as a foreign name at a glance. In casual sentences Koreans often add 이 after a name that ends in a consonant: 케일럽이 왔어 ("Caleb's here").

Spelled letter-by-letter you'd get 캘레브, but Korean media write 케일럽, because the name is written the way it sounds, not the way it's spelled.

  • An l between vowels is written twice (final ㄹ + ㄹ), so Koreans say a clear l instead of a tapped r.
  • The unstressed "uh" vowel is written ㅓ (eo), whatever letter English uses for it.

Questions people ask

How do you write Caleb in Korean?

Caleb is written 케일럽 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: keilleop). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has three syllables: 케 + 일 + 럽.

How do you pronounce Caleb in Korean?

Say it ke-il-leop, with even weight on every syllable. The double ㄹ in 일럽 is a clear English l, as in "hello", not an r.
