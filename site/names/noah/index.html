Noah in Korean 노아

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Noah as 노아: two syllables, said no-a. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Noah in Hangul
노아
Revised Romanization: noa · said no-a · two syllables

Noah letter by letter

Each Hangul block is one syllable, built from a consonant, a vowel and sometimes a final consonant underneath.

노
ㄴ n + ㅗ o
no
아
ㅇ silent + ㅏ a
a
Pronunciation tip

ㅗ is a pure "o" with rounded lips. Don't add the w-glide English puts in "go".

  • ㅇ at the top of 아 is silent, so 아 is just the vowel: a.
  • Two even beats, no-a: Korean doesn't stress one syllable over the other the way English does.

How Koreans would call you

Friends calling you over
노아야!
노아 ends in a vowel, so it takes 야. Casual, for close friends.
At work or with new people
노아 씨
노아 ssi. Polite, roughly "Mr" or "Ms", but used with the first name.
Customer, client, online
노아 님
노아 nim. What you'll see on a delivery box or a booking.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables, so 노아 already sounds natural to Korean ears. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 노아 or 노아 씨.

Good to know

노아 is also how Korean Bibles write Noah, as in 노아의 방주 (Noah's ark).

Why it's spelled 노아

Here the letters and the sound line up: applying the loanword rules letter by letter already gives 노아, the same spelling Korean media use.

  • The final h is silent and is dropped.

Questions people ask

How do you write Noah in Korean?

Noah is written 노아 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: noa). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has two syllables: 노 + 아.

How do you pronounce Noah in Korean?

Say it no-a, with even weight on every syllable. ㅗ is a pure "o" with rounded lips. Don't add the w-glide English puts in "go".
