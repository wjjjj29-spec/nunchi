Lisa is the maknae (youngest member) of BLACKPINK. In Korean groups, age order matters: the eldest often looks after everyone, and the maknae gets teased and spoiled in equal measure.

What would you call Lisa?

In Korean you don't use the name alone for someone older. If you are younger than Lisa, a woman would say unnie (언니) and a man would say noona (누나). If you are older, Lisa would be calling you that. Born the same year? You're 동갑 (dong-gap), and first names are fine.
