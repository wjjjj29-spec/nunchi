Pronunciation tip

ㄹ between two vowels (캐럴) is one quick tap of the tongue, like the tt in American "butter": closer to a soft d than an English r.

  • The double ㄹ in 럴라 is a clear English l, as in "hello", not an r.
  • ㅓ (eo) in 럴 is the open "uh" of "sun", not an "o".
  • ㅐ (ae) is said like the e in "bed" by most Koreans today, a little more closed than the a in "cat".
  • ㅋ at the start is aspirated: a clear puff of air, like the k in "kite".

Korean given names are almost always two syllables (after a one-syllable family name), so at 4 syllables 캐럴라인 reads as a foreign name at a glance. In casual sentences Koreans often add 이 after a name that ends in a consonant: 캐럴라인이 왔어 ("Caroline's here").

Why it's spelled 캐럴라인

How Koreans write it
캐럴라인
the settled media spelling

Spelled letter-by-letter you'd get 카롤라인, but Korean media write 캐럴라인, because the name is written the way it sounds, not the way it's spelled.

  • The final e is silent. It only makes the vowel before it long.
  • An l between vowels is written twice (final ㄹ + ㄹ), so Koreans say a clear l instead of a tapped r.
  • The unstressed "uh" vowel is written ㅓ (eo), whatever letter English uses for it.

Questions people ask

How do you write Caroline in Korean?

Caroline is written 캐럴라인 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: kaereollain). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has four syllables: 캐 + 럴 + 라 + 인.

How do you pronounce Caroline in Korean?

Say it kae-reol-la-in, with even weight on every syllable. ㄹ between two vowels (캐럴) is one quick tap of the tongue, like the tt in American "butter": closer to a soft d than an English r.
