How Koreans would call you

Friends calling you over

에비 ends in a vowel, so it takes 야. Casual, for close friends.
At work or with new people
에비 씨
에비 ssi. Polite, roughly "Mr" or "Ms", but used with the first name.
Customer, client, online
에비 님
에비 nim. What you'll see on a delivery box or a booking.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables, so 에비 already sounds natural to Korean ears. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 에비 or 에비 씨.

Why it's spelled 에비

Here the letters and the sound line up: applying the loanword rules letter by letter already gives 에비, the same spelling Korean media use.

  • There is no v in Korean either: v becomes ㅂ (b).

Questions people ask

How do you write Evie in Korean?

Evie is written 에비 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: ebi). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has two syllables: 에 + 비.

How do you pronounce Evie in Korean?

Say it e-bi, with even weight on every syllable. Two even beats, e-bi: Korean doesn't stress one syllable over the other the way English does.
