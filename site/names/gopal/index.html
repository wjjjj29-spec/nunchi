Gopal in Korean 고팔

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Gopal as 고팔: two syllables, said go-pal. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Gopal letter by letter

Each Hangul block is one syllable, built from a consonant, a vowel and sometimes a final consonant underneath.

고

go
팔

Pronunciation tip

ㄹ at the bottom of 팔 is an l: the tongue touches just behind the teeth and stays there.

  • ㄱ at the start is soft and unvoiced, halfway between k and g.
  • ㅗ is a pure "o" with rounded lips. Don't add the w-glide English puts in "go".
  • Two even beats, go-pal: Korean doesn't stress one syllable over the other the way English does.

고팔 nim. What you'll see on a delivery box or a booking.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables, so 고팔 already sounds natural to Korean ears. In casual sentences Koreans often add 이 after a name that ends in a consonant: 고팔이 왔어 ("Gopal's here").

Questions people ask

How do you write Gopal in Korean?

Gopal is written 고팔 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: gopal). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has two syllables: 고 + 팔.

How do you pronounce Gopal in Korean?

Say it go-pal, with even weight on every syllable. ㄹ at the bottom of 팔 is an l: the tongue touches just behind the teeth and stays there.

What does Gopal mean in Korean?

On its own, nothing: 고팔 spells the sound of Gopal, so the syllables are not Korean words and Koreans read it simply as a foreign name.
