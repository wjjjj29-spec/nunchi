Manon in Korean 마농

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Manon as 마농: two syllables, said ma-nong. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Pronunciation tip

ㅇ at the bottom of 농 is "ng" as in "sing". At the top of a syllable ㅇ is silent.

  • ㅗ is a pure "o" with rounded lips. Don't add the w-glide English puts in "go".
  • Two even beats, ma-nong: Korean doesn't stress one syllable over the other the way English does.

Spelled letter-by-letter you'd get 매논, but Korean media write 마농, because the name is written the way it sounds in French, not the way it's spelled.

  • Korean writes French names the French way: nasal vowels like 'an/on' end in ㅇ or ㄴ.

Questions people ask

How do you write Manon in Korean?

Manon is written 마농 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: manong). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name and follows the French pronunciation. In Hangul it has two syllables: 마 + 농.

How do you pronounce Manon in Korean?

Say it ma-nong, with even weight on every syllable. ㅇ at the bottom of 농 is "ng" as in "sing". At the top of a syllable ㅇ is silent.
