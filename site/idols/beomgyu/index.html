What would you call Beomgyu?

In Korean you don't use the name alone for someone older. If you are younger than Beomgyu, a woman would say oppa (오빠) and a man would say hyung (형). If you are older, Beomgyu would be calling you that. Born the same year? You're 동갑 (dong-gap), and first names are fine.
