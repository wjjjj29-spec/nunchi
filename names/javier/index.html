Javier in Korean 하비에르

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Javier as 하비에르: four syllables, said ha-bi-e-reu. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Pronunciation tip

ㄹ between two vowels (에르) is one quick tap of the tongue, like the tt in American "butter": closer to a soft d than an English r.

  • 르 carries the filler vowel ㅡ (eu). Keep it short and light, almost just the consonant.
  • ㅇ at the top of 에 is silent, so 에 is just the vowel: e.
  • Give all 4 syllables the same weight. Korean has no strong stress, so it's ha-bi-e-reu, evenly.

How Koreans would call you

Friends calling you over
하비에르야!
하비에르 ends in a vowel, so it takes 야. Casual, for close friends.
At work or with new people
하비에르 씨
하비에르 ssi. Polite, roughly "Mr" or "Ms", but used with the first name.
Customer, client, online
하비에르 님
하비에르 nim. What you'll see on a delivery box or a booking.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables (after a one-syllable family name), so at 4 syllables 하비에르 reads as a foreign name at a glance. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 하비에르 or 하비에르 씨.

Spelled letter-by-letter you'd get 재버, but Korean media write 하비에르, because the name is written the way it sounds in Spanish, not the way it's spelled.

  • Korean writes Spanish names by their Spanish sound: vowels are read as written, j is an h sound (ㅎ) and the r is pronounced (ㄹ/르) instead of being dropped as in English.

Questions people ask

How do you write Javier in Korean?

Javier is written 하비에르 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: habiereu). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name and follows the Spanish pronunciation. In Hangul it has four syllables: 하 + 비 + 에 + 르.

How do you pronounce Javier in Korean?

Say it ha-bi-e-reu, with even weight on every syllable. ㄹ between two vowels (에르) is one quick tap of the tongue, like the tt in American "butter": closer to a soft d than an English r.
