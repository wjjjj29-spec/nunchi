Jonas in Korean 요나스

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Jonas as 요나스: three syllables, said yo-na-seu. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Pronunciation tip

스 carries the filler vowel ㅡ (eu). Keep it short and light, almost just the consonant.

  • Give all 3 syllables the same weight. Korean has no strong stress, so it's yo-na-seu, evenly.

요나스 nim. What you'll see on a delivery box or a booking.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables (after a one-syllable family name), so at 3 syllables 요나스 reads as a foreign name at a glance. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 요나스 or 요나스 씨.

How Koreans write it

Spelled letter-by-letter you'd get 조내스, but Korean media write 요나스, because the name is written the way it sounds in German, not the way it's spelled.

  • Korean follows the German sound: j is a y sound (Jonas → 요나스) and vowels are read as written.

Questions people ask

How do you write Jonas in Korean?

Jonas is written 요나스 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: yonaseu). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name and follows the German pronunciation. In Hangul it has three syllables: 요 + 나 + 스.

How do you pronounce Jonas in Korean?

Say it yo-na-seu, with even weight on every syllable. 스 carries the filler vowel ㅡ (eu). Keep it short and light, almost just the consonant.
