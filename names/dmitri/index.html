Pronunciation tip

ㄹ between two vowels (트리) is one quick tap of the tongue, like the tt in American "butter": closer to a soft d than an English r.

  • 드 and 트 carry the filler vowel ㅡ (eu). Keep them short and light, almost just the consonant.
  • ㄷ at the start is soft and unvoiced, halfway between t and d.
  • Give all 4 syllables the same weight. Korean has no strong stress, so it's deu-mi-teu-ri, evenly.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables (after a one-syllable family name), so at 4 syllables 드미트리 reads as a foreign name at a glance. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 드미트리 or 드미트리 씨.

Why it's spelled 드미트리

Here the letters and the sound line up: applying the loanword rules letter by letter already gives 드미트리, the same spelling Korean media use.

  • The Korean spelling comes from the Russian (Cyrillic) form, so it follows Russian pronunciation rather than the English letters.

Questions people ask

How do you write Dmitri in Korean?

How do you pronounce Dmitri in Korean?

Say it deu-mi-teu-ri, with even weight on every syllable. ㄹ between two vowels (트리) is one quick tap of the tongue, like the tt in American "butter": closer to a soft d than an English r.
