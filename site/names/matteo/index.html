Matteo in Korean 마테오

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Matteo as 마테오: three syllables, said ma-te-o. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Pronunciation tip

ㅗ is a pure "o" with rounded lips. Don't add the w-glide English puts in "go".

  • ㅇ at the top of 오 is silent, so 오 is just the vowel: o.
  • Give all 3 syllables the same weight. Korean has no strong stress, so it's ma-te-o, evenly.

How Koreans would call you

Friends calling you over

마테오 ends in a vowel, so it takes 야. Casual, for close friends.
At work or with new people
마테오 씨
마테오 ssi. Polite, roughly "Mr" or "Ms", but used with the first name.
Customer, client, online
마테오 님
마테오 nim. What you'll see on a delivery box or a booking.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables (after a one-syllable family name), so at 3 syllables 마테오 reads as a foreign name at a glance. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 마테오 or 마테오 씨.

Why it's spelled 마테오

Here the letters and the sound line up: applying the loanword rules letter by letter already gives 마테오, the same spelling Korean media use.

  • Korean follows the Italian sound: every vowel is pronounced and double consonants are written once.

Questions people ask

How do you write Matteo in Korean?

Matteo is written 마테오 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: mateo). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name and follows the Italian pronunciation. In Hangul it has three syllables: 마 + 테 + 오.

How do you pronounce Matteo in Korean?

Say it ma-te-o, with even weight on every syllable. ㅗ is a pure "o" with rounded lips. Don't add the w-glide English puts in "go".
