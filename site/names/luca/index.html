Luca in Korean 루카

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Luca as 루카: two syllables, said ru-ka. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Luca letter by letter

Each Hangul block is one syllable, built from a consonant, a vowel and sometimes a final consonant underneath.

Pronunciation tip

The opening ㄹ is a single light tap, halfway between an English r and l. Never roll it.

  • Two even beats, ru-ka: Korean doesn't stress one syllable over the other the way English does.

How Koreans would call you

Friends calling you over
루카야!
루카 ends in a vowel, so it takes 야. Casual, for close friends.
At work or with new people
루카 씨
루카 ssi. Polite, roughly "Mr" or "Ms", but used with the first name.
Customer, client, online
루카 님
루카 nim. What you'll see on a delivery box or a booking.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables, so 루카 already sounds natural to Korean ears. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 루카 or 루카 씨.

Why it's spelled 루카

Here the letters and the sound line up: applying the loanword rules letter by letter already gives 루카, the same spelling Korean media use.

  • Korean follows the Italian sound: every vowel is pronounced.

Questions people ask

How do you write Luca in Korean?

Luca is written 루카 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: ruka). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name and follows the Italian pronunciation. In Hangul it has two syllables: 루 + 카.

How do you pronounce Luca in Korean?

Say it ru-ka, with even weight on every syllable. The opening ㄹ is a single light tap, halfway between an English r and l. Never roll it.
