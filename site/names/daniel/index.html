Daniel in Korean 대니얼

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Daniel as 대니얼: three syllables, said dae-ni-eol. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Pronunciation tip

ㄹ at the bottom of 얼 is an l: the tongue touches just behind the teeth and stays there.

  • ㅓ (eo) in 얼 is the open "uh" of "sun", not an "o".
  • ㅐ (ae) is said like the e in "bed" by most Koreans today, a little more closed than the a in "cat".
  • ㄷ at the start is soft and unvoiced, halfway between t and d.
  • ㅇ at the top of 얼 is silent, so 얼 is just the vowel: eol.

How Koreans would call you

Friends calling you over
대니얼아!
대니얼 ends in a consonant, so it takes 아, said 대니어라. Casual, for close friends.
At work or with new people
대니얼 씨
대니얼 ssi. Polite, roughly "Mr" or "Ms", but used with the first name.
Customer, client, online
대니얼 님
대니얼 nim. What you'll see on a delivery box or a booking.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables (after a one-syllable family name), so at 3 syllables 대니얼 reads as a foreign name at a glance. In casual sentences Koreans often add 이 after a name that ends in a consonant: 대니얼이 왔어 ("Daniel's here").

Spelled letter-by-letter you'd get 대닐, but Korean media write 대니얼, because the name is written the way it sounds, not the way it's spelled.

  • The unstressed "uh" vowel is written ㅓ (eo), whatever letter English uses for it.

Questions people ask

How do you write Daniel in Korean?

Daniel is written 대니얼 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: daenieol). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has three syllables: 대 + 니 + 얼.

How do you pronounce Daniel in Korean?

Say it dae-ni-eol, with even weight on every syllable. ㄹ at the bottom of 얼 is an l: the tongue touches just behind the teeth and stays there.
