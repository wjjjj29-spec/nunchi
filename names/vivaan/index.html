Vivaan in Korean 비반

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Vivaan as 비반: two syllables, said bi-ban. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Vivaan letter by letter

Pronunciation tip

ㅂ at the start is soft and unvoiced, halfway between p and b.

  • Two even beats, bi-ban: Korean doesn't stress one syllable over the other the way English does.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables, so 비반 already sounds natural to Korean ears. In casual sentences Koreans often add 이 after a name that ends in a consonant: 비반이 왔어 ("Vivaan's here").

Spelled letter-by-letter you'd get 비바안, but Korean media write 비반, because the name is written the way it sounds, not the way it's spelled.

  • There is no v in Korean either: v becomes ㅂ (b).

Questions people ask

How do you write Vivaan in Korean?

Vivaan is written 비반 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: biban). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has two syllables: 비 + 반.

How do you pronounce Vivaan in Korean?

Say it bi-ban, with even weight on every syllable. ㅂ at the start is soft and unvoiced, halfway between p and b.

What does Vivaan mean in Korean?

On its own, nothing: 비반 spells the sound of Vivaan, so the syllables are not Korean words and Koreans read it simply as a foreign name.
