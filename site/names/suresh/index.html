Suresh in Korean 수레시

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Suresh as 수레시: three syllables, said su-re-si. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Pronunciation tip

ㄹ between two vowels (수레) is one quick tap of the tongue, like the tt in American "butter": closer to a soft d than an English r.

  • The final 시 stands for the English "sh" sound. Say the i very lightly.
  • Give all 3 syllables the same weight. Korean has no strong stress, so it's su-re-si, evenly.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables (after a one-syllable family name), so at 3 syllables 수레시 reads as a foreign name at a glance. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 수레시 or 수레시 씨.

Why it's spelled 수레시

Here the letters and the sound line up: applying the loanword rules letter by letter already gives 수레시, the same spelling Korean media use.

  • A final "sh" sound gets the vowel ㅣ (시) rather than ㅡ.

Questions people ask

How do you write Suresh in Korean?

Suresh is written 수레시 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: suresi). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has three syllables: 수 + 레 + 시.

How do you pronounce Suresh in Korean?

Say it su-re-si, with even weight on every syllable. ㄹ between two vowels (수레) is one quick tap of the tongue, like the tt in American "butter": closer to a soft d than an English r.

What does Suresh mean in Korean?

On its own, nothing: 수레시 spells the sound of Suresh, so the syllables are not Korean words and Koreans read it simply as a foreign name.
