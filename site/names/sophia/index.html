Sophia in Korean 소피아

Korean media, baristas and the immigration office write Sophia as 소피아: three syllables, said so-pi-a. Here's how it's built, how to say it, and what people will call you.

Sophia in Hangul
소피아
Revised Romanization: sopia · said so-pi-a · three syllables

Same Hangul as Sofia: Korean spells the sound, so different English spellings can land on the same 소피아.

Pronunciation tip

ㅗ is a pure "o" with rounded lips. Don't add the w-glide English puts in "go".

  • ㅇ at the top of 아 is silent, so 아 is just the vowel: a.
  • Give all 3 syllables the same weight. Korean has no strong stress, so it's so-pi-a, evenly.

How Koreans would call you

Friends calling you over
소피아야!
소피아 ends in a vowel, so it takes 야. Casual, for close friends.
At work or with new people
소피아 씨
소피아 ssi. Polite, roughly "Mr" or "Ms", but used with the first name.
Customer, client, online
소피아 님
소피아 nim. What you'll see on a delivery box or a booking.

Korean given names are almost always two syllables (after a one-syllable family name), so at 3 syllables 소피아 reads as a foreign name at a glance. With foreign names some Koreans skip 야 and just say 소피아 or 소피아 씨.

Why it's spelled 소피아

Here the letters and the sound line up: applying the loanword rules letter by letter already gives 소피아, the same spelling Korean media use.

  • ph is an f sound, and Korean has no f, so it becomes ㅍ (p).

Questions people ask

How do you write Sophia in Korean?

Sophia is written 소피아 in Hangul (Revised Romanization: sopia). It is the standard spelling Korean media use for the name. In Hangul it has three syllables: 소 + 피 + 아.

How do you pronounce Sophia in Korean?

Say it so-pi-a, with even weight on every syllable. ㅗ is a pure "o" with rounded lips. Don't add the w-glide English puts in "go".
